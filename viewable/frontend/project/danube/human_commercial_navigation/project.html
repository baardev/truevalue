description: 'The Danube is Europe''s second longest river and the busiest international
  waterway after the Rhine, carrying approximately 34 million tonnes of cargo per
  year on the 2,414 km navigable section from Kelheim (Germany) to the Black Sea,
  including the Rhine-Main-Danube Canal link to Western Europe. The navigable channel
  is managed under the 1948 Belgrade Convention by the Danube Commission (13 member
  states) and is classified as a core Trans-European Transport Network (TEN-T) corridor.
  Annual transport value is estimated at EUR 5-8 billion/yr in trade facilitation.
  The principal operator nations are Austria, Slovakia, Hungary, Croatia, Serbia,
  Bulgaria, Romania, Ukraine, and Moldova. The primary bottleneck is Phase 2 (Fairway
  Maintenance and Dredging, 72% balance): critical shallows at the Iron Gate gorge
  exit (Romanian-Serbian border) and the Bratislava-Budapest reach regularly constrain
  vessel drafts to 1.5-2.0 m in low-flow years, well below the 2.5 m design depth
  of the TEN-T standard. Climate change is lengthening low-flow periods, making dredging
  an increasingly expensive and ecologically damaging maintenance activity. This subproject
  pairs with natural_river_channel to form the paired_navigation composite.
