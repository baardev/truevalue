description: 'The human supply chain covering catch effort planning, active fishing
  operations, processing and cold chain, market access, and revenue capture for Danube
  basin freshwater commercial fisheries. The basin supports approximately 25,000 commercial
  fishing households, predominantly in Romania, Hungary, Serbia, Ukraine, and Moldova.
  Annual commercial catch is approximately 22,000 tonnes of fish, valued at EUR 145
  million at first sale. Aquaculture (predominantly carp and catfish) contributes
  a further 90,000 tonnes per year, mainly in Hungary, Romania, and Serbia, valued
  at EUR 210 million. The chain faces severe structural pressures: sturgeon commercial
  fishing has been nationally banned or severely restricted since 2006-2019 in all
  Danube states, removing the highest-value species from legal trade. Illegal poaching
  of sturgeon and other species remains significant in Romania, Serbia, Ukraine, and
  Moldova. The primary bottleneck is Phase 2 (Processing and Cold Chain) at 72% balance,
  where fragmented small-scale processing infrastructure and cold chain gaps result
  in high post-catch losses (estimated 18-25% in non-EU states) and limit market access
  for premium product grades. This subproject pairs with natural_fish_population to
  form the paired_fisheries composite.
