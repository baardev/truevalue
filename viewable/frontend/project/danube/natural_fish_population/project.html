description: 'The Danube and its tributaries support approximately 100 native fish
  species, the highest freshwater fish diversity of any European river system. The
  natural fish population service cascade runs from habitat provision (spawning grounds,
  floodplain nurseries, migration corridors) through juvenile recruitment, adult biomass
  accumulation, and harvest-ready population stock. Total commercial and recreational
  fishable biomass is estimated at 420,000 tonnes across the basin. The cascade faces
  four converging pressures: 60 dams blocking migration routes (most critically the
  Iron Gate I and II), 47% loss of floodplain nursery habitat, heavy eutrophication
  in the middle and lower basin (40% of water bodies failing WFD good ecological status),
  and overfishing pressure in the lower basin and delta. The primary bottleneck is
  Phase 1 (Habitat and Migration), where dam fragmentation has severed 84% of the
  historical migratory fish range. This subproject pairs with human_commercial_fishing
  to form the paired_fisheries composite.

  '
