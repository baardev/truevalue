description: 'The Danube Delta reed industry is the largest commercial reed harvesting
  operation in Europe and one of the largest in the world. Annual commercial harvest
  is approximately 120,000-150,000 tonnes of dry reed from the Danube Delta Biosphere
  Reserve, plus an additional 30,000-40,000 tonnes from the lower Danube floodplain.
  The primary product is thatching reed (Phragmites australis) sold to European and
  North American markets for roof thatching at EUR 1.20-1.80 per bundle. Secondary
  products include reed board for construction insulation, biogas substrate, paper
  pulp, and horticultural mulch. The principal operator is the state-owned SC Deltaprod
  SRL (Romania), with a growing private sector. The industry employs approximately
  4,500 seasonal workers, predominantly from Tulcea and Constanta counties. The primary
  bottleneck is Phase 3 (Market Access and Certification, 72% balance): only 35% of
  harvested reed reaches premium thatching markets; the remainder is sold as low-value
  bulk product. A verified sustainability certification (equivalent to FSC for reed)
  does not yet exist, limiting access to high-value European and UK thatching markets.
  This subproject pairs with natural_reed_bed to form the paired_reed_harvesting composite.

  '
