description: 'Training experiment that adds the tholonic balance regularizer (paper
  Section 11.2) to a standard training objective. Penalizes D-C imbalance at every
  layer simultaneously. Matched pairs (with / without regularizer) trained from the
  same random seed and initialization. Tests whether tholonic structural constraint
  produces faster convergence, better OOD robustness, and reduced specification gaming
  compared to an unregularized baseline.

  '
regularizer:
  formula: L_total = L_task + lambda * sum_l |sigma_D_l - 0.5 * sigma_C_l|^2
  target_ratio: 1:2 (D:C) — mirrors virial theorem equilibrium condition
  sigma_D_l: RMS activation magnitude of D-sublayer outputs at layer l (normalization
    sublayer)
  sigma_C_l: RMS activation magnitude of C-sublayer outputs at layer l (attention/MLP
    sublayer)
  lambda: 0.01
  note: 'The 1:2 D:C target ratio is motivated by the virial theorem (paper §11.2):
    for a bound physical system in equilibrium, <T> = -1/2 <V>, where T is kinetic
    energy (C-role) and V is potential energy (D-role). This is identified as the
    universal tholonic equilibrium condition. The specific value of lambda is a hyperparameter
    requiring tuning.

    '
