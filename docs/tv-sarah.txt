Notes: Major gold mining companies include Newmont, Barrick Gold, Agnico Eagle Mines,
Gold Fields, and AngloGold Ashanti, leading the industry by market cap and production,
alongside significant players like China&#39;s Zijin Mining, while Royal Gold, Franco-Nevada, and
Wheaton Precious Metals focus on royalties and streaming. These companies operate
globally, with some diversifying into copper or focusing on large-scale production, while
smaller firms explore new deposits.
Major Gold Miners (by Market Cap/Production)
 Newmont Corporation: World&#39;s largest gold miner, operating across continents.
 Barrick Gold: A giant in the industry, strategically expanding into copper.
 Agnico Eagle Mines: Strong performance, particularly from Canadian mines.
 Gold Fields: A leading South African-based miner.
 AngloGold Ashanti: Significant South African producer with global assets.
 Zijin Mining: China&#39;s largest gold and copper producer.
 Polyus: A major Russian gold producer.
 Kinross Gold: Canadian company with diverse global operations. 
Royalty &amp; Streaming Companies (Precious Metals)
 Wheaton Precious Metals: Focuses on financing mines for a share of their output.
 Franco-Nevada Corporation: A leading royalty and stream company.
 Royal Gold: Invests in gold mining assets for royalties. 
Other Notable Miners &amp; Explorers
 Eldorado Gold, Alamos Gold, B2Gold, IAMGOLD, Hecla Mining, Lundin Gold, and
Northern Star Resources are also prominent. 
Key Trends
 Consolidation: Large companies acquire smaller ones for reserves.
 Diversification: Miners increasingly focus on other metals like copper.
 Global Operations: Companies operate mines worldwide, from North America to
Australia, Africa, and South America

The Bristol One City Plan (launched 2019) is a collaborative initiative to make Bristol fair,
healthy, and sustainable by 2050, supported by various public, private, and voluntary
stakeholders. While it operates on a partnership model rather than a traditional, single-
sponsor model, key commercial and strategic partners include: 
 Ameresco Limited and Vattenfall Heat UK Limited: Partners in Bristol City Leap,
investing over £1bn in low-carbon energy infrastructure.
 City Partners: A wide range of institutions, businesses, and organizations, including
members of thematic boards focused on the economy, environment, and, transport, as
well as the Bristol Advisory Committee on Climate Change (BACCC).
 UK Core Cities: Collaborating with regional and national partners for investment. 
The initiative aims for a sustainable, net-zero city by 2030, with funding often derived from
collaborative ventures like Mission Net Zero, backed by Innovate UK.
