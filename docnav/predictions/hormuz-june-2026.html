Tholonic structural reasoning

Phase cascade structure: Phases 1 and 2 are Coherent because both parties have strong structural incentives (D and C roughly balanced, C slightly dominant). The system becomes stressed at Phase 3 because Iran's incentive to retain Hormuz leverage flips the D-C balance: C drops while D rises. Phase 4 then fails not only because Phase 3 is stressed, but because Phase 4 has its own independent physical impossibility given the time constraint.

The 74-point crowd gap is structurally correct: Polymarket prices deal text at 93% and Hormuz normalization at 19%. The N-D-C model explains exactly why: Phases 1-2 are coherent (high upstream success probability) and Phases 3-4 are stressed-to-critical (low downstream delivery probability). The crowd has correctly intuited the phase cascade failure even without the structural vocabulary to name it.

The Phase 4 time constraint is the binding variable: For the IMF Portwatch 7-day moving average to reach 60 by June 30, normalization must begin no later than June 24. Maritime insurance markets (Lloyds, P&I clubs) do not reopen on 7-day timelines after geopolitical events of this magnitude. This is a structural impossibility embedded in Phase 4's D-side, independent of whether Phase 3 resolves. Even if Iran signs a full Hormuz terms agreement tomorrow (June 18), Phase 4 operational normalization by June 30 is structurally near-impossible on lead-time grounds alone.

If the July 31 market resolves "No" (below 56%), the model's Phase 3 D-overshoot reading (Iran retaining leverage even after text release) would gain evidential support.
