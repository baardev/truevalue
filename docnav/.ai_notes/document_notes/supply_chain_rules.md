## One-Sentence Summary

Defines the rules for modeling the physical supply-chain layer before adding value-chain or financial interpretation.

## Key Claims

- Physical flow, custody, constraints, and measurable outputs must be mapped before value-chain interpretation.
- Supply-chain modeling should avoid pricing, margins, financial leverage, and benefit allocation.
