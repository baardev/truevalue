# Blue Economy

## Definition

Economic activity connected to oceans and coastal systems that depends on ecological health, sustainable livelihoods, and marine governance.

## Why It Matters

Blue Economy work requires integrating ecological, social, market, and financial layers without collapsing them into one metric.
