# Baseline Data

## Definition

Baseline data is the measured starting condition of a system before intervention, including ecological, social, governance, market, and financial reference points.

## Why It Matters

Blue Carbon cannot demonstrate improvement, additionality, or outcome-linked performance without baselines for habitat condition, carbon stocks, sequestration, fisheries, livelihoods, and governance.
