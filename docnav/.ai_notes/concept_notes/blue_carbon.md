# Blue Carbon

## Definition

Blue carbon refers to carbon stored or sequestered by coastal and marine ecosystems such as mangroves, seagrasses, salt marshes, and related coastal habitats.

## Why It Matters

Blue carbon can connect ecological restoration, climate mitigation, community livelihoods, biodiversity, coastal protection, and finance. In this project, it must be analyzed through explicit layers: ecological function first, then value-chain interpretation, then financial-abstraction design.
