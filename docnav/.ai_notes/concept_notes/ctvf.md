# cTVF

## Definition

cTVF refers to the Clarity True Value Framework, used here as a system-first diagnostic approach that examines coherence, constraints, contribution, lifecycle maintenance, and investibility prerequisites before final monetized interpretation.

## Why It Matters

For Blue Carbon, cTVF helps distinguish between a project that is narratively attractive and a project that has enough ecological, social, governance, market, and finance evidence to support investment analysis.
