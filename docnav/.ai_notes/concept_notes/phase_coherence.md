# Phase Coherence

## Definition

The stability of a phase when its Definition constraints and Contribution outputs remain aligned enough to produce a coherent Negotiation state.

## Why It Matters

Phase coherence lets the AI evaluate whether a phase is structurally healthy rather than merely described narratively.
