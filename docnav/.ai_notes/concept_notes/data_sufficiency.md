# Data Sufficiency

## Definition

Data sufficiency is the condition in which available evidence is detailed, sourced, measurable, and structured enough to support the analytical claim being made.

## Why It Matters

The Blue Carbon package currently has enough data for gap analysis, but not enough data for cTVF scoring, TVPCI scoring, coupon advice, or investment conclusions.
