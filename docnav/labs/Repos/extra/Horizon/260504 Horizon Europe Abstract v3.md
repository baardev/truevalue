## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**TrueVal-Chains** is a 54-month, €6 million Research and Innovation
Action (RIA) submitted under Horizon Europe Call
HORIZON-CL6-2026-01-CIRCBIO-09: *Balancing food security, bioeconomy,
climate and biodiversity objectives to unlock sustainable value chains*.

### The Problem

The EU\'s bioeconomy agenda --- backed by the CRCF Regulation (2025),
the Nature Credits Roadmap (2025), and the revised CAP --- has created a
regulatory architecture that demands one thing above all else:
**reliable, supply-chain-resolved quantitative evidence** on how
agricultural value, environmental performance, and food security
interact across the farm-to-market chain. That evidence does not yet
exist at the resolution policymakers and markets need. Without it,
carbon farming certification risks greenwashing, nature credits cannot
be priced credibly, farmers remain price-takers unable to monetise their
stewardship, and land-use conflict assessments remain speculative.

### The Solution

This project develops, validates and deploys Clarity's **True Value
Framework (cTVF)** --- a quantitative, phase-resolved supply chain
intelligence methodology built to produce exactly this evidence. cTVF
maps each commodity supply chain into discrete, measurable phases, each
defined by a physical state, a transformation, a measurable output, and
a transparency classification. Across each phase, the framework accounts
simultaneously for physical flows, economic value distribution, Scope
1/2/3 GHG emissions, natural capital (biodiversity, water, soil, carbon
sequestration and other aspects) and social equity --- generating the
integrated accounts that the CRCF, Nature Credits, and EU EAA frameworks
require but currently lack.

### Three Commodity Systems --- One Comparative Framework

The project applies the cTVF to three strategically chosen commodity
systems that together span the full range of governance environments,
supply and service chain architectures, and policy challenges relevant
to the call:

**1. Shea --- West Africa to EU** (anchor commodity)\
Mapping the full supply chain from parkland agroforestry in West Africa
through to EU cosmetics and chocolate manufacturers. Shea is the most
direct link between the Great Green Wall (GGW) --- the AU/EU\'s flagship
\$33 billion Sahel restoration programme, currently only \~2% achieved
--- and EU corporate Scope 3 obligations under CSRD. The project
delivers the first phase-resolved Scope 3 GHG emission factors for shea,
unlocking carbon and nature credit revenue for 4--6 million women
cooperative members along the GGW corridor.

**2. Olive Oil --- Spain and Italy** (EU cooperative anchor)\
Spain is the world\'s largest olive oil producer; cooperatives account
for \~65% of production. Italy is the critical bulk trade destination
--- the Spain→Italy value flow is a textbook case of value appropriation
asymmetry. The project delivers a cooperative business model innovation
assessment, olive mill waste circular bioeconomy analysis, natural
capital accounts for Andalusian olive systems, and policy
recommendations benchmarked against the OECD\'s existing Spain policy
recommendations. The ROBIN Horizon Europe project (Andalusia governance)
is identified as a direct complementary partner.

**3. cTVF Diversification Commodities --- Argentine Soya/Beef and
Spanish Cherries**\
Tests cTVF scalability across a high-deforestation-risk Global South
commodity (Argentine soya/beef, directly relevant to the EU
Deforestation Regulation) and a predominantly domestically bounded EU
horticultural system (Spanish cherries). Together these generate the
cross-governance comparative dataset needed to make the project\'s
policy recommendations universally applicable, not case-specific.

### Methodology

The cTVF is grounded in the **Negotiation--Definition--Contribution
(N-D-C) triadic analytical structure** --- a formally validated
framework converging the unique mathematical constants found in human
and natural systems. The cTVF models each supply chain phase as a system
of constraints (D), outputs (C), and emergent stable state (N).
Sustainability is measured as the D/C balance: imbalance reveals
structural fragility, value leakage, or environmental risk. The
framework has been developed and validated on the global gold supply
chain (Phases 0--7) and now extended to agricultural commodity systems.
TVF is directly compatible with SEEA natural capital accounting, IPBES
biodiversity valuation, the GHG Protocol Scope 3 standard, and the EU
Economic Accounts for Agriculture (EAA) framework.
