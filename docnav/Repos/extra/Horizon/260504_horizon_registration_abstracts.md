## Part B: Full Abstract (target 1,000 to 2,000 characters)

> The EU bioeconomy agenda demands reliable quantitative evidence on how agricultural value, environmental performance, and food security interact across the farm-to-market chain. That evidence does not yet exist at the resolution that policymakers, carbon markets, and corporate buyers require.
>
> TrueVal-Chains develops, validates and deploys the Clarity True Value Framework (cTVF), a phase-resolved supply chain analytics methodology, across three strategically chosen commodity systems: West African shea butter, Spanish and Italian olive oil, and a diversification pair of Argentine soya/beef and Spanish cherries. Each supply chain is mapped into discrete, measurable phases that simultaneously track physical flows, GHG emissions (Scope 1/2/3), natural capital, economic value distribution, and social equity.
>
> The cTVF is grounded in the Negotiation-Definition-Contribution (N-D-C) triadic model, a formally validated analytical structure that measures systemic sustainability as the balance between constraints and outputs at each supply chain phase. It is directly compatible with the CRCF Regulation (2025), the EU Nature Credits Roadmap, IPBES biodiversity valuation standards, and the EU Economic Accounts for Agriculture.
>
> Expected impacts include: the first phase-resolved Scope 3 GHG emission factors for shea, unlocking carbon and nature credit revenue for 4 to 6 million women cooperative members along the Great Green Wall corridor; cooperative business model innovations for EU olive oil producers; deforestation-free supply chain evidence for Argentine soya/beef under the EU Deforestation Regulation; and policy recommendations for CAP, CRCF, and Nature Credits, developed with a co-created Multi-Actor Panel of farmers, cooperatives, manufacturers, and policymakers across five countries.
