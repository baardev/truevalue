### 3. LEAR Appointment and Validation

Each organization needs a designated Legal Entity Appointed Representative (LEAR). This is the person authorized to sign legally on behalf of the organization in the portal. LEAR appointment runs in parallel with organization validation but requires physical signed documents submitted to the REA.

Required documents per organization:
- LEAR appointment letter (system-generated, must be signed and stamped by the legal representative)
- Proof of identity of the LEAR
- Proof of empowerment (that the LEAR has authority to act)

**Lead time:** Runs in parallel with PIC validation. Allow 6 to 8 weeks from initiation. For non-EU organizations this can take longer due to document authentication requirements.

**High-risk partners for LEAR:** TrueValue Analytics (if newly constituted), Serious Shea SARL (Burkina Faso, document authentication from a non-EU jurisdiction adds time), CIFOR-ICRAF Benin (international organization status may require specific documentation).

### 5. Consortium Eligibility: Country and Partner Count

The call requires a minimum of 3 EU Member States or Associated Countries and an implied minimum of 7 partners (as stated in the proposal).

Current EU/Associated country coverage:
- Spain: TrueValue Analytics (Alicante), Cooperativas Agro-alimentarias, University of Malaga
- Belgium: CEPS
- Italy: CREA
- France: ImpactAgri (if selected over Serious Shea)

**Minimum threshold met:** Yes. Four distinct EU Member States with 5 to 6 EU-based partners. This is solid.

**Third-country partner eligibility for EU funding:**

Argentina (CIPPEC), Burkina Faso/Benin (Serious Shea, CIFOR-ICRAF) are not EU Associated Countries. Under Horizon Europe general rules, third-country participants can join the consortium but are not automatically eligible to receive EU funding unless:
- Their country is specifically listed as eligible in the Work Programme for this call, or
- They are classified as a low- or lower-middle-income country (which applies to Burkina Faso and Benin, not Argentina), or
- They self-fund their participation

**Action required:** Check the specific Work Programme text for call HORIZON-CL6-2026-01-CIRCBIO-09 to confirm whether Argentina is listed as a funded third country. If not, CIPPEC must either self-fund or the budget must reflect that they receive no EU contribution. CIFOR-ICRAF and Serious Shea (Burkina Faso/Benin) are more likely to qualify under lower-income country provisions, but this must be confirmed.

## Feasibility Assessment

**Can the deadline be met?** Yes, if action starts now on the highest-priority items.

**The two items most likely to cause a missed deadline:**

1. PIC registration and LEAR validation for TrueValue Analytics and Serious Shea SARL. If these are not initiated in the next two to three weeks, the 6 to 8 week validation window will eat into the August document finalization period.

2. Third-country funding eligibility. If Argentina is not listed as a funded third country in the Work Programme, the budget and consortium structure must be adjusted. Finding this out in August instead of May could require a significant rewrite.

**The item most likely to cause a post-submission problem:**

Ethics self-assessment. If the proposal triggers an ethics review (likely given the data collection from women cooperatives in Burkina Faso and Benin), this is flagged during evaluation, not at submission. But a poorly completed self-assessment can slow or complicate the evaluation. Do it properly in July.
