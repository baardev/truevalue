🧭 TVPCI — True Value Pricing Convergence Index

The TVPCI measures how closely a commodity's market price converges with its theoretical TrueValue across five mathematical models. Each model is anchored to a fundamental constant and captures a different dimension of value coherence — from structural definition (π) through emergent integration (e). Together they form a pentagon signature that reveals where market pricing diverges from underlying reality.

π — Structural Definition φ — Organic Growth √2 — Diagonal Tension ln(2) — Information Decay e — Emergent Integration
