Operating Principles (9 Rule Sets)

  • Separation of Concerns (physical → value → financial)
  • Phase-Based Modeling (everything maps to phases)
  • Data-First Discipline (no speculation, mark opacity)
  • Transparency Classification (high/medium/low)
  • Custody Awareness (ownership ≠ custody ≠ control)
  • Exchange Data as Anchor (COMEX = reconciliation target)
  • Schema-First Development (all insights tabulated)
  • Deferred Interpretation (map terrain before value)
  • Reproducibility & Auditability (traceable claims)
