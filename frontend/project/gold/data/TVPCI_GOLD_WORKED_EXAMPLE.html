1 Overview and Approach

This document applies every formula from the TVPCI specification to the gold supply chain — from geological prospecting to exchange-registered bullion. The goal is to demonstrate exactly what the index measures, how it is computed, and why a single under-performing phase can dominate the final score.

2 Phase Inputs: D, C, B, S, N

For each phase we compute five quantities using the following formulas:

B
Balance score — maximum when D = C, decays as imbalance grows
$$B_i = 100 \cdot \exp\!\left(-2 \cdot \frac{|D_i - C_i|}{\max(D_i,\, C_i)}\right)$$
S
Sustainability index — energy cost of D-C imbalance
$$S_i = \frac{100}{|D_i - C_i|^2 + 10}$$
N
N-state — emergent operational capacity of the phase
$$N_i = \sqrt{D_i \cdot C_i} \cdot \frac{B_i}{100}$$

Phase 6 (Vaulting) — Full Calculation

Phase 6 is the critical case. Working through each formula with \(D_6 = 292\), \(C_6 = 190\):

1
Balance score B₆
$$B_6 = 100 \cdot \exp\!\left(-2 \cdot \frac{|292 - 190|}{\max(292,\,190)}\right) = 100 \cdot \exp\!\left(-2 \cdot \frac{102}{292}\right) = 100 \cdot e^{-0.699} = \mathbf{49.7}$$

The phase is operating at roughly half its structural potential due to the D-C imbalance alone.

2
Sustainability index S₆
$$S_6 = \frac{100}{102^2 + 10} = \frac{100}{10{,}414} = \mathbf{0.010}$$

Near-zero: the imbalance is extremely energy-costly. Compare to Phase 5 (Casting): S₅ = 100/(100 + 10) = 0.909. Phase 6 is 91× less sustainable per unit of output.

3
N-state N₆
$$N_6 = \sqrt{292 \times 190} \cdot \frac{49.7}{100} = \sqrt{55{,}480} \cdot 0.497 = 235.5 \times 0.497 = \mathbf{117}$$

Phase 5 produced N = 244. Phase 6 delivers N = 117. The N-state is more than halved at the vaulting boundary — this is the suppression event that dominates the final TVPCI score.

3 Phase Boundary Coherence (φ-scores)

For each adjacent pair of phases we compute the N-state ratio \(r\) and compare it to the golden ratio \(\varphi = 1.618\). The φ-score decays exponentially as the ratio deviates from \(\varphi\) in either direction.

$$r_{i \to i+1} = \frac{N_{i+1}}{N_i} \qquad\qquad \Phi_{i \to i+1} = 100 \cdot \exp\!\left(-2 \cdot \frac{|r - \varphi|}{\varphi}\right)$$

Why φ = 1.618? When D and C propagate through phases under the Tholonic Fibonacci inheritance rule, the ratio of consecutive geometric-mean N-states converges to \(\varphi\). A ratio at φ means value is being passed forward in natural proportion; any deviation signals disrupted inheritance — suppression (r ≪ φ) or disproportionate recovery (r ≫ φ).

4 Chain-Level Aggregates

4.1 Mean Phase Balance (\(\bar{B}\))

$$\bar{B} = \frac{78.7 + 85.1 + 92.1 + 86.5 + 91.3 + 92.8 + 49.7 + 89.1}{8} = \frac{665.3}{8} = \mathbf{83.2}$$

Without Phase 6, the mean of the remaining seven phases would be 87.9 — a meaningfully stronger chain. Phase 6's B = 49.7 accounts for the full gap.

4.2 System φ-Coherence (\(\bar{\Phi}\))

$$\bar{\Phi} = \frac{74.1 + 48.7 + 39.9 + 53.3 + 44.4 + 24.5 + 57.9}{7} = \frac{342.8}{7} = \mathbf{49.0}$$

The P5→P6 boundary (24.5) alone pulls this average down by roughly 3.5 points. No boundary in the chain achieves the 80+ "near-φ" band — the gold chain lacks proportional handoffs throughout, not only at vaulting, though vaulting is the worst.

4.3 Phase Sustainability Normalised (\(\bar{S}_{\text{norm}}\))

Raw sustainability values per phase:

$$S = [\,0.110,\; 0.157,\; 0.649,\; 0.244,\; 0.559,\; 0.909,\; 0.010,\; 0.376\,] \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \bar{S} = 0.377$$

Normalise using \(S_{\min} = 0.010\) (Phase 6) and \(S_{\max} = 0.909\) (Phase 5):

$$\bar{S}_{\text{norm}} = \frac{0.377 - 0.010}{0.909 - 0.010} \cdot 100 = \frac{0.367}{0.899} \cdot 100 = \mathbf{40.8}$$

Phase 6 sets both the normalisation minimum and compresses the scale. It is 91× less sustainable per unit of structural output than Phase 5.

Before opacity correction: 61.1 → Moderate Divergence (55–74). Identifiable structural phases are distorting the index. True Value is partially obscured. The bottleneck is unambiguously Phase 6 (Vaulting).

6 Opacity Correction

Phase 0 (Prospecting) is OPAQUE: exploration D and C parameters cannot be independently verified from public data. The specification requires a transparency penalty to reflect this epistemic limit:

$$\text{TVPCI}_{\text{adj}} = 61.1 \times \left(1 - 0.15 \times \frac{1}{8}\right) = 61.1 \times 0.981 = \mathbf{59.9}$$
ℹ️
This is not a penalty for wrongdoing. Prospecting data is opaque for sound commercial reasons. The 1.2-point adjustment acknowledges that the chain cannot be fully verified — a small but structurally honest correction.

8 What This Means in Practice

Structural Strengths
Phases P2 (Processing), P4 (Refining), and P5 (Casting) all score B ≥ 91. These phases add genuine operational capacity. Refining benefits from enforced LBMA standards; Casting from unambiguous physical specifications.
Primary Bottleneck: Vaulting
Phase 6 (Vaulting) carries B = 49.7. The security/compliance constraint structure (D) absorbs far more capacity than the vault network (C) returns. Correcting Phase 6 alone would raise the TVPCI by an estimated 10–15 points.
No Boundary Achieves Near-φ Health
The system φ-coherence is 49.0. Even the best boundary (P0→P1, Φ = 74.1) falls short of the 80+ "near-φ" band. The chain functions — but no phase-to-phase handoff is proportionally ideal.
What TVPCI 59.9 Means for Price
The TVPCI does not produce a price. It states: 40 structural index points of friction, imbalance, and incoherence exist in this chain. Any gold price formed here carries an unexplained structural component of that magnitude. Its direction — overpricing, underpricing, or redistribution — is a Layer 4 question.

Counterfactual: What if Vaulting Were Fixed?

Suppose a structural intervention increased Phase 6's vault network integration — raising C from 190 toward 240 (improved logistics, distributed vault access), while D remains unchanged:

A 15–17 point improvement is achievable from structural change in a single phase. This is the TVPCI's diagnostic purpose: not to assign blame, but to identify exactly where targeted intervention produces the largest structural gain.

✓
Structural intervention as a policy instrument. Any change that raises C₆ (vault network integration, distributed custody, transport connectivity) without proportionally raising D₆ (additional regulatory constraints) moves the gold chain toward structural coherence. The TVPCI provides the quantitative baseline against which such interventions can be measured before and after.
