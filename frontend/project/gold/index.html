Five-Model N·D·C Analysis

Gold — Five-Model Coherence Signature

Each vertex represents a Tholonic model derived from a mathematical constant. The outer zone (>61.8%, φ⁻¹) is coherent; the middle zone (38.2–61.8%) is failure; the inner zone (<38.2%, 1−φ⁻¹) is breakdown. A score of 0 means unmeasured, not failed.

Phase-Level Detail

Gold — Phase-by-Phase N·D·C Profile

Each axis is one supply chain phase. The three polygons show Definition (D), Contribution (C), and Negotiation (N) at each phase. Where D and C are far apart, the phase is under stress. Where N sits close to both, the phase is in healthy equilibrium.

PHI Sustainability Analysis

Gold — Phase Balance Score with PHI Zone Rings

Each axis is one supply chain phase. The single polygon shows the balance score (0–100%) per phase. The two zone rings mark the φ-derived structural thresholds: 61.8% sustainability floor (φ⁻¹) and 38.2% breakdown threshold (1−φ⁻¹).
