Five-Model N·D·C Analysis

Gold — Five-Model Coherence Signature

Each vertex represents a Tholonic model derived from a mathematical constant. The outer zone (70–100%) is coherent; the middle zone (40–70%) is stressed; the inner zone (<40%) indicates systemic failure in that dimension. A score of 0 means unmeasured, not failed.

Gold — Phase-by-Phase N·D·C Profile

Each axis is one supply chain phase. The three polygons show Definition (D), Contribution (C), and Negotiation (N) at each phase. Where D and C are far apart, the phase is under stress. Where N sits close to both, the phase is in healthy equilibrium.
