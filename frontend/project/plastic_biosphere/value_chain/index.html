e-Axis Context

The Finance Coupling Score: 38.5 (Breakdown edge)

The e-axis score of 38.5 is the most diagnostically important signal in this project's coherence profile. It sits at the edge of the Breakdown zone (below 38.2%) and reflects a near-total structural disconnection between available financial instruments and measurable physical outcomes in the ocean remediation space.

Estimated dedicated ocean plastic remediation funding globally is approximately $1.5 billion per year (2024). To match the current accumulation rate at current removal efficiency (approximately 5,000 tonnes/year removed vs. approximately 8 million tonnes/year entering the ocean), would require approximately 1,600x current funding, or approximately $2.4 trillion per year. The ratio of current funding to required funding produces the e-axis score.

No standardized ocean plastic bond KPI framework exists as of 2026. The Global Plastics Treaty (UNEA INC process) is ongoing but has not yet produced binding production caps. The plastic credit market (Verra Plastic Standard, Ocean Bound Plastic) is growing but lacks a connection to ocean concentration metrics. The e-axis captures this structural gap: capital exists in the world economy, but no instrument reliably directs it toward measurable ocean plastic outcomes.
