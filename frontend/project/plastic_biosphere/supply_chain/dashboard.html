Bottleneck: Phase 7 (Trophic Transfer and Bioaccumulation, 50.5%). Phases 5-7 all enter the Failure zone (below 61.8%) because the transition from monitored marine-surface dispersal to deep-sea and biological phases crosses a transparency cliff: measurement coverage drops below 5%, no remediation technology exists, and corrective biological responses (plastisphere bacteria) operate at rates orders of magnitude below the accumulation rate. Phase 7 is the deepest in Failure because it combines the lowest D-C ratio (95 vs. 188) with zero operational corrective mechanisms and the lowest data quality in the chain. The structural cause is not a single-phase problem: it is the progressive loss of custody and observability as plastic crosses the thermocline boundary.
