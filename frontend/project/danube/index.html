Pilot Subprojects — Phase 2

Active Pilot: Agricultural Water Pair

The largest water-use sector in the basin: 22 km³/yr (63% of total abstraction), 2.8 million hectares. The natural component is the hydrological freshwater asset; the human component is the irrigation infrastructure supply chain (intake → primary canal → secondary distribution → field application → drainage). Current efficiency 50% — a performance bond targeting 65% could save 5.5 km³/yr.
