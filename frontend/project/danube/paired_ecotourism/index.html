Joint Ecotourism Bond Opportunity

This paired system has a distinctive bond structure: both components share an inter-dependent failure mode. The delta's ecological quality (natural asset) drives visitor demand; cultural heritage management (human chain) captures and reinvests visitor revenue. Deterioration in either component reduces the other: habitat degradation reduces ecotourism appeal; under-investment in heritage reduces visitor spend and return rates.
