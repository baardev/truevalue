Danube Basin · Natural System · Floodplain Forest Biomass

💰 Value Chain Overview

e = 22
Abstract / Financial Coupling Score

The floodplain forest biomass system has a very partial financial abstraction layer (e = 22). Three active carbon projects under the Verra VCS registry provide a nascent outcome-linked mechanism, but they capture only 0.05 Mt CO2/yr against a 1.0 Mt/yr potential. ERDF restoration grants are input-based (reporting expenditure, not outcomes). EU LIFE project grants are milestone- linked but non-standardised. No performance bond currently connects measured connectivity restoration or verified carbon additionality to investor returns.

A Floodplain Reconnection Bond indexed to lateral connectivity improvement (0.55 to 0.70) and verified carbon additionality (rising from 0.05 to 1.0 Mt CO2/yr over 10 years) would shift the e-layer from near-zero to 55-60. The revenue basis is substantial: EU ETS at EUR 60-80/tCO2 implies EUR 60-80M/yr at full potential. The financial coupling problem is one of project registration and measurement design, not of carbon market availability.
