Danube Basin · Natural System

Floodplain Forest Biomass Natural Paired with Floodplain Forestry

The Danube floodplain corridor supports 160,000 ha of floodplain forest (47% of historical extent), accumulating 17 Mt CO2 in carbon stock and sequestering 1.8 Mt CO2/yr. This subproject models the ecological production function from hydrological connectivity through soil formation, canopy development, biomass accumulation, and ecosystem service delivery. Chain balance 81.0%. Phase 0 (hydrological connectivity) is the structural bottleneck at 79%: the annual flood pulse that builds and sustains floodplain forests has been reduced 40% by dams and 2,800 km of embankments.
