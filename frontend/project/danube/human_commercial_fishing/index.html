Danube Basin · Human System

🎣 Human Commercial Fishing Human Paired with Fish Population

The Danube commercial fishing sector supports 25,000 fishing households across 10 riparian states, harvesting 22,000 t/yr of legal catch (plus an estimated 4,600 t illegal). Total sector value is EUR 355M/yr including EUR 210M/yr aquaculture. Chain balance 76.2%. Phase 2 (Processing, Cold Chain, and Preservation) is the primary bottleneck at 72%: cold chain coverage is only 58%, post-catch losses reach 21% in non-EU states, and processing recovery of 62% is below the 72% EU benchmark.

Bottleneck: Phase 2: Processing, Cold Chain, and Preservation (72% balance)
Cold chain coverage is 58% basin-wide; 42% of catch in the lower Danube and non-EU states is transported without refrigeration. Post-catch losses reach 21% in non-EU states (vs 8% in EU), destroying EUR 35-45M/yr of value from a EUR 355M/yr sector. Processing recovery rate of 62% is below the European inland fisheries benchmark of 72%. Phase 4 (Revenue Capture, 74%) is also weak: only 8% of sector income is reinvested in conservation (benchmark: 20%); EUR 5,800/yr average household income competes poorly with alternative livelihoods.

Natural pair: Natural Fish Population (78.0% balance)

The natural fish population is the biological capital base for commercial fishing operations. The bond that restores fish pass infrastructure (natural Phase 1) unlocks the stock recovery that human Phase 1 (harvest operations) depends on. The two bottlenecks are independent and can be addressed simultaneously as separate tranches of a dual instrument. See: Natural Fish Population →  |  Fisheries Pair Hub →
