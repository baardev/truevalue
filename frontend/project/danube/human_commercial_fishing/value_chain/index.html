Abstract / Financial Coupling Score

The commercial fishing sector has a weak financial abstraction layer (e = 24). EMFAF 2021-2027 provides EUR 320M over seven years in co-financing, but as an input-based grant programme it reports expenditure milestones rather than measured outcomes (cold chain coverage, food safety certification rate, post-catch loss reduction). TRAFFIC seizure data is not monetised as a conservation finance instrument. No performance bond currently links measured post-catch loss reduction or food safety certification improvements to investor returns in any Danube country.

A Fisheries Value Chain Bond indexed to cold chain coverage (58% to 75%), food safety certification rate (52% to 70%), and post-catch loss rate (21% to 12% in non-EU states) would shift e from 24 toward 45-55. The revenue basis is concrete: EUR 35-45M/yr of value currently destroyed by inadequate cold chain would flow directly to sector revenue after infrastructure investment. The financial coupling problem is one of instrument design and co-financing structure, not of underlying sector viability.
