Phase Lifecycle N-D-C

Structural Interpretation

Phase 2 (72%) is the primary structural bottleneck: D (cold chain and food safety requirements for value preservation) substantially exceeds C (actual cold chain coverage and processing recovery delivered). 42% of catch crosses the gap between vessel and first-sale point without refrigeration. The D-C imbalance is worst in Romania, Serbia, Ukraine, and Moldova, where cold chain infrastructure is either absent or intermittent. At 21% post-catch loss in non-EU states, the sector is destroying EUR 35-45M/yr of value that infrastructure investment would directly recover. Phase 4 (74%) is also weak: sector reinvestment in conservation at 8% of revenue is less than half the 20% benchmark, meaning the sector does not fund its own long-term resource base.

Phase 1 (80%, D approximately equal to C) is the system resilience node: active fishing operations produce 22,000 t/yr of legal catch across 10 countries, with floodplain CPUE of 31 kg/trip vs 18 kg/trip in the main channel confirming the ecosystem service value of intact floodplain habitat. The 480,000 recreational licences and EUR 85M recreational sector value add to the Phase 1 resilience signal. This phase is functional despite the ecological degradation inherited from the natural system. The 71% gear compliance rate and 78% quota utilisation indicate an operational sector that responds to basic regulatory signals.

A Fisheries Value Chain Bond targeting Phase 2 cold chain and food safety infrastructure would directly address the D-C imbalance at the primary bottleneck. Cold chain coverage rising from 58% to 75% (year 5) would reduce post-catch losses from 21% to below 12% in non-EU states, recovering EUR 20-30M/yr of currently destroyed value. Food safety certification rising from 52% to 70% would unlock export market access worth EUR 15-25M/yr in premium pricing. The e-score of 24 reflects that EMFAF grants provide partial financing but no performance bond for fisheries value chain infrastructure exists. A verified outcome bond indexed to cold chain coverage and food safety certification would shift e from 24 toward 45-55.
