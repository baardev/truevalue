Bond opportunity: The primary bonding opportunity is a performance bond structured around Phase 3 (distribution network) rehabilitation in lower basin countries. Target: reduce NRW from 25% to 15% across Romania, Moldova, Ukraine, and Bosnia over a 10-year period.

Performance trigger: NRW measured annually via smart metering and audit. Bond coupon stepped: full coupon paid when NRW reduction on track; partial coupon if below target; coupon holiday if NRW rises above baseline.

Financial basis: Tariff revenue in EU member states provides senior repayment security. EU Cohesion Fund co-financing reduces junior tranche risk. EIB guarantee mechanism available for eligible EU member states.

Natural asset coupling: NRW reduction directly reduces pressure on the paired natural freshwater availability asset. Every 1% reduction in NRW at 35 km³ abstraction saves ~350 million m³ — reducing ecological extraction pressure and improving Phase 4 of the natural service cascade.
