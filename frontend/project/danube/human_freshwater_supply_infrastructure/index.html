Key structural issue: Phase 3 (Distribution Network) is the chain bottleneck with 81% balance — the lowest in the chain. Non-revenue water averaging 25% (up to 40% in parts of Romania, Moldova, Ukraine) represents both a financial liability and increased pressure on the paired natural asset. Network rehabilitation investment would be the optimal bond-financed intervention.
Paired with: Natural Freshwater Availability — the natural hydrological asset this infrastructure draws on. Abstraction (35 km³) vs. renewable (206 km³): stock ratio 0.17 — currently sustainable, but increasing under climate change. Bond risk: drought reduces supply while infrastructure costs remain fixed.
