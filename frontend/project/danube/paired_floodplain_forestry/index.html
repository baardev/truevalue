Joint Floodplain Forestry Bond Opportunity

This paired system has a uniquely linked bond structure: the natural and human bottlenecks are causally connected. Natural Phase 0 (flood connectivity) bottleneck directly suppresses the carbon additionality that human Phase 3 (carbon market access) requires to register credits. A bond that fixes one without the other captures only partial value. The Floodplain Reconnection Bond (natural) and the Carbon Market Development Bond (human) must be designed as a single dual-tranche instrument to capture the full 1.0 Mt CO2/yr carbon revenue potential.
