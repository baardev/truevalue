The Danube floodplain forestry sector manages 140,000 ha under formal management plans, harvesting 800,000 m3/yr of timber across Austria, Hungary, Croatia, Romania, Serbia, Bulgaria, and Ukraine. Total sector output includes 210,000 m3/yr sawnwood and 1,200 GWh/yr biomass energy. Carbon credit potential is 1,000,000 tCO2/yr but actual registration stands at only 50,000 tCO2/yr. Bottleneck: Phase 3: Market Access and Certification (73% balance)
Only 45% of harvest volume carries FSC/PEFC certification. The carbon credit gap is 20-fold: 50,000 tCO2/yr actual vs 1,000,000 tCO2/yr potential. Non-EU states (Serbia, Ukraine) have certification rates of 8% and 5% respectively and face EUDR compliance risk from 2025. Without carbon project registration and certification scale-up, EUR 60-70M/yr in carbon revenue remains uncaptured. Phase 4 (Revenue Capture, 70%) is the weakest link: EUR 48/ha/yr forest revenue against EUR 185/ha/yr agricultural conversion value sustains deforestation pressure.
