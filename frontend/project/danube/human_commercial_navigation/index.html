Europe's second longest river carries 34 Mt/yr of cargo worth EUR 6.5 bn/yr across 2,414 km of navigable waterway, linking 13 Danube Commission member states through 68 active ports and a fleet of 3,200 commercial vessels. Chain balance: 79.2%. River transport emits 5 g CO2/tkm versus 90 g CO2/tkm by road (18x climate advantage). Average low-water restriction days: 42/yr; 68 days in 2022. Cargo diverted by restrictions: 8 Mt/yr, equivalent to 320,000 additional truck movements on road. Annual dredging cost: EUR 28M/yr, rising year-on-year. Fairway compliance rate: 74% (7 critical shallows remain below minimum navigable depth). Climate trend: low-water days are increasing at 1.5 days/yr.
Climate projection: At current trend, restriction days will exceed 60/yr by 2040 unless nature-based fairway management (side-arm reconnection, sediment rebalancing) is implemented alongside or in place of mechanical dredging. The Iron Gate sediment deficit is the structural driver.
