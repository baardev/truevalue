🌾 Irrigation Infrastructure Human Paired

The engineered irrigation supply chain abstracting 22 km³/yr from the Danube system to irrigate 2.8 million hectares of agricultural land. Largest single water-use sector (63% of total basin abstraction). Critical bond opportunity: efficiency improvement from 50% to 70% saves 5.5 km³/yr.

Key structural issue: Phase 2 (Secondary Distribution Network) is the chain bottleneck with 77% balance, the lowest in the chain. Farm-level metering covers only 35% of irrigated area, creating a major opacity gap (score 4/5). The 85,000 km of largely unlined secondary canals loses an average 15% before water reaches the farm gate. Canal rehabilitation and metering rollout are the optimal bond-financed interventions.
Paired with: Natural Freshwater Availability — the hydrological asset this infrastructure draws on. Agricultural abstraction (22 km³) represents 10.7% of basin runoff (206 km³). Currently sustainable at basin scale, but several sub-basins (Upper Tisza, Lower Prut) already show seasonal stress. Bond risk: drought years reduce available abstraction while fixed infrastructure costs remain. See Agricultural Water Hub for the paired composite index.
