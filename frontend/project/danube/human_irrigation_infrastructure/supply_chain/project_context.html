Agricultural irrigation is the largest single water-use sector in the Danube Basin, accounting for 22 km³/yr out of 35 km³/yr total abstraction (63%). Romania (3.4 km³/yr) and Bulgaria (2.1 km³/yr) are the largest users, followed by Hungary, Serbia, and Ukraine. Roughly 60% of primary canal infrastructure was constructed between 1950 and 1975 under Soviet and Yugoslav state programmes. Much of it is now 45-50 years old, well past design life, and operating at 45-55% efficiency.[1] The European Union's Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) provides some financial support for rehabilitation in EU member states, but non-EU countries (Ukraine, Serbia, Bosnia) face a large unmet rehabilitation need estimated by the World Bank at approximately €8.4 billion basin-wide.
