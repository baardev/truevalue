Bottleneck: Phase 3: Market Access and Certification (72% balance)
No reed-specific sustainability certification standard exists anywhere in the world. EUR 12M/yr in price premium is forgone as a result. The price differential is 6.8x: thatching grade commands EUR 285/t while bulk biomass realises only EUR 42/t. Market access score is 0.42 out of 1.0. Without a recognised certification standard, buyers in the UK (42% of exports), Germany, and the Netherlands cannot pay thatching premiums for certified product regardless of actual harvesting practices.
Climate risk: ice access days declining at 1.8 days/yr. Current: 38 ice-access days per season (was 52 in 1990). At current rate, below 20 days/yr by 2040. Mechanical harvesting requires frozen ground in the Delta; warm winters shift operations toward spring, compressing the harvest window and increasing risk of nesting-season conflicts.
