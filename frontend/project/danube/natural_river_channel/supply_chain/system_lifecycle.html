Channel Morphology and Substrate Function 72.0% ⚠ BOTTLENECK

PHASE 3 · MIDSTREAM · Low Transparency · BOTTLENECK
Sediment Transport and Geomorphic Work 72.0% ⚠ BOTTLENECK
D slightly exceeds C: the river is defined to transport sediment (D=260) but the contribution (actual sediment delivery) is structurally blocked (C=232). Iron Gate I and II trap 57% of the sediment load. Current delivery: 17 Mt/yr vs. pre-regulation 40 Mt/yr. The bed is incising at 8 mm/yr in key reaches because the sediment deficit cannot be met by local erosion alone. The delta is at net loss of 3.7 mm/yr. This is a permanent break: the dams are not being removed, and natural recovery requires centuries without intervention.

PHASE 4 · DOWNSTREAM · Medium Transparency
Flow Delivery to Delta and Black Sea 82.0%
195 km3/yr reaches the Black Sea via three arms: Chilia (58%), Sulina (21%), Sfantu Gheorghe (21%). Nutrient loading is high: 380 kt N/yr and 12 kt P/yr, contributing to Black Sea eutrophication. D slightly exceeds C: the flow is delivered but the sediment-depleted water causes erosion at the delta front rather than building habitat. Nutrient loads are well-monitored; suspended sediment flux has medium transparency due to sampling gaps in the arms.

Five-Model Coherence Pentagon

pi=79Operational coherence. Chain average balance 79.2%. Coherent zone (>61.8%, φ⁻¹). The double bottleneck at Phases 2 and 3 suppresses the score below the ideal but the system remains above the sustainability floor.
phi=52Proportional efficiency. Score 52 (failure, 38.2-61.8). Strong D: the catchment hydrology is well-defined and documented. Weak C: the morphological services (gravel bar substrate, side-arm habitat, floodplain exchange) that the river is supposed to contribute are severely degraded. This is the tholonic signature of the Iron Gate legacy: definition survives, contribution does not.
sqrt2=71Structural overhead. Score 71 (coherent, >61.8%). Moderate overhead from channelisation infrastructure maintaining the navigation corridor. Transfer losses exist but the primary flow pathway is intact.
ln2=58Growth/transformation. Score 58 (failure, 38.2-61.8). Value transformation across phases is constrained: the natural enrichment of the flow as it passes through the channel (sediment pick-up, groundwater exchange, habitat creation) is structurally impaired at Phases 2 and 3.
e=32Financial abstraction. Score 32 (breakdown, <38.2%). Some WFD restoration investment and EU LIFE programme funding exist. No performance bond for channel morphology has been issued. The gap between the magnitude of the structural problem and the financial instruments addressing it is very large.
Lifecycle interpretation: The river channel system is structurally coherent at the hydrological level (Phases 0 and 1 at 85%) but permanently degraded at the geomorphological level (Phases 2 and 3 at 72%). The phi=52 score precisely captures this asymmetry: the river's Definition (what it is and where it goes) remains intact, but its Contribution (what it does for morphological and ecological services) has been severed by the Iron Gate dam cascade. The e=32 score confirms that the financial system has not yet responded at the scale required. A performance bond targeting gravel augmentation, side-arm reconnection, and delta sediment monitoring would address all three dimensions simultaneously: raising C in Phases 2 and 3, increasing the e-score, and creating a measurable performance framework for the WFD targets.
