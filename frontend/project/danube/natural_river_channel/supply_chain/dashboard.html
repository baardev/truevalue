DOUBLE BOTTLENECK: Phases 2 and 3 both at 72%. Iron Gate I (1972) and Iron Gate II (1984) permanently altered the geomorphological regime: 57% of upstream sediment is now trapped, 80% of the channel is channelised, 77% of gravel bars are gone, bed incision is progressing at 8 mm/yr, and the delta is at net loss of 3.7 mm/yr. This is not two separate problems. It is one structural break propagating through two adjacent phases with no natural recovery pathway at the current intervention level.
