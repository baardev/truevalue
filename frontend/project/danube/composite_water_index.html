Rating methodology note: The current composite grade uses a threshold lookup on the average N-D-C balance score. A full production bond rating would additionally apply: (1) cash flow coverage ratios from water tariffs and CAP payments; (2) climate risk modifiers (drought probability under RCP 4.5/8.5 scenarios — Danube runoff projected -10% to -50% by 2050[1]); and (3) governance risk discounts for non-EU countries (Ukraine, Serbia) operating outside the EU WFD and CAP frameworks (extraction cap absent — irrigation efficiency paradox applies[2]). The current score is a structural health grade, not a cash-flow credit rating. Natural freshwater Phase 4 balance adjusted from 90% to 86% (WFD 40% good ecological status; ICPDR 2021[1]). Irrigation e-score adjusted from 28 to 15 (efficiency paradox; 60% of irrigated area in non-EU countries with no financial abstraction[2]).
