Joint Reed Harvesting Bond Opportunity: Danube Delta Wetland Ecosystem Bond

Unlike the fisheries pair (where natural and human bottlenecks are structurally independent), the reed harvesting pair has a single causal root: the absence of a reed sustainability certification standard. This creates an unusually clean bond design: one instrument addresses both chain failures simultaneously. The natural chain cannot monetise its EUR 232M/yr ecosystem services without a certifiable sustainable supply claim. The human chain cannot access EUR 12M/yr in price premiums without the same certification. The bond finances the institutional infrastructure that both chains require.
