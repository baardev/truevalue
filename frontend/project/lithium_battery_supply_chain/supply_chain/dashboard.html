Bottleneck Alert: Phases 1 and 2: Midstream Concentration and Export-Control Risk

Phase 1 (Mineral Refining and Processing, balance 67.2%) has the highest D-value in the chain (D=305) because refining capacity is extremely concentrated in China: approximately 65% of lithium, 75% of cobalt, and over 90% of graphite. Phase 2 (Cathode and Anode Material Production, balance 72.9%, D=295) shows the same pattern one step downstream, with China holding roughly 85% of cathode and over 90% of anode active material capacity. Both phases carry documented and ongoing Chinese export restriction risk on cathode and lithium/graphite processing technology, a structural sqrt2-axis failure that cannot be resolved through cell-manufacturing diversification alone. The D-C gap in Phase 1 (gap = 100) is the primary bond and policy intervention target.
