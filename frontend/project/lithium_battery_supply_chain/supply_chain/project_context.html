Geographic Concentration by Phase

The lithium battery supply chain is geologically distributed at the mining stage but structurally concentrated at the refining and materials stages. This inversion, wide upstream distribution narrowing to a single-country midstream chokepoint, is the defining structural feature of the chain.

Policy and Funding Landscape

Diversification of the lithium battery chain away from single-country midstream concentration is an active but early-stage policy objective across multiple jurisdictions. None of these instruments are yet operationally linked to measurable phase-level diversification KPIs, which is the structural basis of the e-axis (financial abstraction) Stressed score of 60.0.

Phase Intervention Worksheet

PROVISIONAL: Recommendations are derived analytically from PDI N-D-C data and public sources. Field validation is required before capital commitment. Each intervention is grounded in the specific mathematical axis that identifies the type of failure.

How the Five-Model Constants Diagnose Failure

Each of the five coherence axes (pi, phi, sqrt2, ln2, e) measures a different dimension of systemic health. When a phase has a low balance score, the five-model analysis identifies which type of failure is driving it. This determines what category of intervention can actually close the D-C gap, rather than applying generic solutions.

The N-D-C balance formula is: balance = 100 - (|D - C| / max(D, C)) * 100. A failing phase has D significantly greater than C: the constraint load (D) exceeds the system's contribution capacity (C). Each constant axis explains a different reason why D is elevated or C is suppressed.

Phi-derived zone thresholds: Coherent 80 and above; Stressed 61.8-80; Failure 38.2-61.8; Breakdown below 38.2. Only pi (81.5) is Coherent; the remaining four axes (phi, sqrt2, ln2, e) are all in the Stressed zone, below the 80 threshold. Phi and sqrt2 converge on the same two bottleneck phases: 1 (Mineral Refining) and 2 (Cathode and Anode Material Production).

1
Mineral Refining and Processing
Balance 67.2% | D=305, C=205, gap=100 | Primary failure axes: sqrt2 (√2) and phi (φ)
Bottleneck

Why sqrt2 and phi diagnose this failure: The sqrt2 axis measures structural overhead: physical or institutional friction that raises D (the constraint load) without adding to C (the contribution capacity). In Phase 1, the structural overhead is jurisdictional concentration: China refines approximately 65% of global lithium, 75% of global cobalt, and over 90% of global graphite, and has documented and ongoing export restrictions on battery cathode and lithium/graphite processing technology. Any single licensing decision can constrain global supply. This pushes D to 305, the highest in the chain, while C (the geographically diversified contribution capacity that would offset this risk) remains at only 205. The phi axis failure (chain score 62.0, just above the 61.8 Failure boundary) is the second diagnosis: the countries that mine the raw minerals (Chile, Australia, DRC, Indonesia) capture a disproportionately small share of the value that midstream refining and processing generates in China, a resource-curse-pattern value trap at the phase transition between mining and refining.

This would substantially reduce Phase 1 as the chain's weakest point and would raise the sqrt2 and phi chain axes toward the Coherent zone (80 and above) as diversified capacity and value-sharing offtake agreements come online.

2
Cathode and Anode Material Production
Balance 72.9% | D=295, C=215, gap=80 | Primary failure axes: phi (φ) and e
Bottleneck

Why phi and e diagnose this failure: The phi axis failure repeats one step downstream from Phase 1: China holds approximately 85% of cathode active material capacity and over 90% (up to 98% by some estimates) of anode active material capacity, predominantly graphite-based. Material specifications are proprietary and supply contracts are rarely disclosed, meaning value (and the intellectual property that generates it) is captured almost entirely within one jurisdiction rather than being distributed proportionally to contribution. The e axis failure (chain score 60.0, at the edge of the Stressed zone) is the second diagnosis: IRA Section 45X credits and Defense Production Act Title III awards exist and are actively disbursed, but they are not yet formally linked to measurable material-diversification KPIs at the phase level, so their effect on this specific bottleneck cannot yet be verified or tracked.

Combined effect: manufacturing incentives, offtake agreements, and feedstock stockpiling together would bring Phase 2 from 72.9% to an estimated 80-83%, above the chain pi score (81.5%) and into the Coherent zone. At that point Phase 1 (Mineral Refining) would remain the chain's sole structural bottleneck, since materials diversification depends on refined feedstock diversification as a pre-condition.
