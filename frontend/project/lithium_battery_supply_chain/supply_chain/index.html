Lithium Battery Supply Chain: Supply Chain Analysis

6-phase N-D-C chain from mining (lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite) through mineral refining, cathode/anode material production, cell manufacturing, pack assembly and deployment (EV and grid storage), and second-life and recycling. Chain average balance: 81.5% (Good zone). Bottleneck phases: Phase 1 (Mineral Refining and Processing, 67.2%) and Phase 2 (Cathode and Anode Material Production, 72.9%). Structural cause: extreme geographic concentration of midstream refining and materials processing in China, compounded by documented export-control policy risk.
