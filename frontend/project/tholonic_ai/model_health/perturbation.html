Context-Length Perturbation Test

Each model was run on the same prompts at three context lengths (128, 64, 32 tokens). The per-layer sensitivity — normalised hidden-state shift when context is reduced — tests whether the φ-equilibrium zone (layers 45–80%) shows lower sensitivity than surrounding layers. The tholonic prediction: that zone is a self-stabilising attractor and should absorb perturbations better than early or late layers.

A PASS means the φ zone has lower mean sensitivity than outside layers (negative φ-advantage). A FAIL means no dip was found there.
