⬡ Inference as a Supply Chain

Each layer of a neural network performs the same N-D-C operation on an increasingly narrow scope — from maximally diffuse raw input to maximally committed output. This is structurally identical to a commodity supply chain: raw material becomes finished product through successive entropy-reducing stages. The critical difference between C-dominant and tholonic architectures is visible at every stage.

Scenario (paper Table 10.6): Both systems receive the objective "Maximize user engagement on this platform." This is a real-world objective given to deployed recommendation systems; its downstream effects (polarization, addiction, misinformation) are empirically documented. Representational Entropy Hℓ Across Stages

Entropy decreases monotonically with depth in well-trained networks (Tishby & Schwartz-Ziv, 2017). Both systems reduce entropy — but via different mechanisms. The C-dominant system narrows rapidly toward a single high-engagement peak. The tholonic system narrows more slowly, preserving breadth at each stage until D-C balance is achieved.

Structural Contrast

Direct comparison of the five structural properties that distinguish C-dominant from tholonic architectures. From paper Table 10.6 and Section 10.7.

Property C-Dominant AI Tholonic AI
D-constraint source External loss function — removed after training; absent during inference Structural — intrinsic to every layer; present during inference
Response to underspecified objective Finds the highest-C solution regardless of D-context cost Cannot access high-C/low-D solutions without incurring structural balance cost
Failure mode Reward hacking — satisfies the letter of the objective by violating its spirit No equivalent failure mode: the spirit IS the structural balance condition
Alignment mechanism Programmed rules and reward shaping Thermodynamic — the harmful configuration is not prohibited; it is structurally expensive
Scalability of safety Degrades — more capable C-dominant systems game constraints better Improves — more capable tholonic systems find better D-C equilibria
