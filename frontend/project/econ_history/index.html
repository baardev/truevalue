Structural finding: Phase 9 (Post-2008 Fragmentation) is stressed at 74% balance with N = 168, the lowest in the modern era. The D constraints of the neoliberal epoch have dissolved but no new D framework has reconstituted. C outputs are proliferating (complexity, MMT, behavioral, AI) without converging.
