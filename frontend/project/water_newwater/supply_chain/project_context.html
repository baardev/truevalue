Project Overview

Singapore PUB's NEWater programme is the world's most advanced example of full-cycle water recycling operated by a single public utility. PUB (Public Utilities Board) acts as sole operator, regulator, and beneficiary across all 12 phases of the water cycle — from catchment management through potable treatment, consumer use, used water collection, and NEWater advanced treatment back to reservoir replenishment.

The system has operated continuously since 2003 and supplies approximately 40% of Singapore's national water demand (2024), with a target of 55% by 2060. NEWater independently exceeds WHO drinking water guidelines — independently verified through 46,000+ annual quality tests.

⚖️ Tholonic model note: The single-actor institutional structure is the key variable in the Tholonic comparison with OCWD GWRS. Zero inter-institutional handoffs in the recycling loop (Phases 7–11) means D and C are set by the same actor at every phase boundary, eliminating coordination overhead and enabling the lower treatment cost of USD 0.88/m³ vs OCWD's USD 1.25/m³. Tholonic N-D-C Framework Summary

Single-actor dominance: PUB is the sole actor at all 12 phases. This eliminates inter-institutional D-C discontinuities in the recycling loop (Phases 5–11). All B9 flags (custody changes) in Events 8–12 are false except the consumer delivery event (Event 6: PUB → consumer) and the consumer wastewater event (Event 7: consumer → PUB). The institutional simplicity is reflected in consistently high balance scores for recycling phases.


Transparency profile: Zero low-transparency phases. The only medium-transparency phases are those with physical inaccessibility (underground distribution, service reservoirs) — not institutional opacity. This is structurally distinct from OCWD which has one low-transparency phase (service reservoir) driven by actor fragmentation.


TVPCI implication: The cost of institutional overhead is approximately zero at Phase 8 (advanced treatment) because PUB receives its own secondary effluent and applies advanced treatment within the same organisational boundary. By contrast, OCWD's Phase 8 incurs the OC San→OCWD supply agreement cost — an institutional overhead that accounts for approximately USD 0.37/m³ of the total USD 0.37/m³ treatment cost premium. The Tholonic model predicts this as a direct consequence of the D-C discontinuity at the inter-actor boundary.

How the Five-Model Constants Diagnose Failure: Advanced Water Recycling (NEWater-type System)

Each coherence axis (pi, phi, sqrt2, ln2, e) measures a different dimension of systemic health. The N-D-C balance formula is balance = 100 - (|D - C| / max(D, C)) * 100. A failing phase has D significantly greater than C: the constraint load exceeds contribution capacity. Chain average balance: 87.6%.
