TVPCI Insight — Why USD 0.88/m³?

The NEWater treatment cost of USD 0.88/m³ reflects the lower institutional overhead of a single-actor system. PUB operates the entire chain — zero inter-institutional supply agreements, zero inter-agency compliance overhead, and direct loop closure from Phase 11 back to Phase 0. The Tholonic model predicts: lower D-C discontinuity → higher chain-level N stability → lower cost. By contrast, multi-actor groundwater-reuse systems can carry higher institutional overhead from inter-agency supply agreements and fragmented distribution networks.
