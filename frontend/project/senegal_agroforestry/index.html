Structural Bottleneck: Processing Transformation (Phase 4)
The Transformation phase (Phase 4, balance 65%) is the primary constraint in this chain. The 18 distributed Multi-functional Clean Energy Processing Centres co-produce commodities (moringa powder, shea paste, groundnut oil) alongside energy outputs (biogas/syngas, biochars) with no centralised throughput reporting. This structural opacity at the highest-volume conversion point propagates uncertainty upward to the refining and export phases. Phase 1 (Harvest, 70%) is a secondary bottleneck driven by informal cooperative-level harvest records. Phase 5 (Refining, 70%) lacks published data on the 2 Shea Butter Processing Clusters. Strengthening data flows at these three phases is the prerequisite for activating bond-linked ecosystem finance instruments.
