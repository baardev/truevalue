Moringa
Metrics Pending
Products: leaf powder, moringa oil, seed extract, fodder biomass. Markets: nutraceuticals, food ingredient, medicinal. 18 processing centres produce moringa powder as a primary output. No price or volume data extracted.
Groundnut
Metrics Pending
Senegal's premier cash crop. Key buyer: SONACOS (domestic state-linked processor). Export market: industrial oil pressing. Extraction yield: ~50 kg oil per 100 kg raw peanuts. Press cake as livestock feed. SavanaNut (Saint-Louis) as commercial operator.
