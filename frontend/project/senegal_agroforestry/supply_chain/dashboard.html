Bottleneck Alert: Phase 4 — Transformation (balance 65%)
Phase 4 is the primary bottleneck: the 18 distributed Multi-functional Clean Energy Processing Centres co-produce commodities and energy (biogas/syngas, biochars) with no centralised throughput reporting. This structural opacity propagates upstream uncertainty to Phases 5 and 6. Secondary bottlenecks: Phase 1 (Harvest, 70%) has informal cooperative-level records; Phase 5 (Refining, 70%) lacks published data from the 2 Shea Butter Processing Clusters. Resolving Phase 4 opacity is prerequisite for activating bond-linked ecosystem finance.
