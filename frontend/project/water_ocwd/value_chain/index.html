⚠️ TVPCI Insight — Why USD 1.25/m³?

OCWD's treatment cost of USD 1.25/m³ reflects institutional overhead despite using standard MF+RO+UV technology. The Tholonic model locates the cost premium in the D-C discontinuity at the OC San → OCWD institutional boundary (Phase 7/8). Each institutional handoff adds coordination overhead — supply agreements, compliance interfaces, dual governance — that elevates D without proportional C increase. The USD 0.37/m³ gap is the measurable institutional efficiency cost. See full TVPCI derivation →
