Tholonic N-D-C: Ecological Return Chain Framework

The primary TVPCI chain measures forward flow: how efficiently constraints (D) and contributions (C) balance to create equilibrium (N) at each phase. The Ecological Return Chain (TVPCI-R) applies the same framework to the reverse: how well each phase recovers, recycles, or regenerates waste into system inputs, closing the loop.

Note on scope: The TVPCI (True Value Pricing Convergence Index) is a comprehensive framework encompassing living wage measurement, provenance integrity, custody transparency across all chain phases, and environmental accounting. The recycling/return chain analysis here represents one application of the TVPCI: the ecological return and waste valorisation dimension. It does not represent the full TVPCI. For the complete definition, see the TrueValue Analytics website front page.

Cocoa generates substantial waste at every phase. The most significant by volume is cocoa pod husks at the farm level: approximately 10 kg of pod husk is generated for every 1 kg of dry cocoa beans. In Ivory Coast alone, this represents approximately 22 million tonnes of pod husk per year, of which the vast majority is burned in place or left to decompose. Cocoa shell at destination processing adds another 10-12% of bean weight as a byproduct. These waste streams are largely unrecovered.

Establishing cooperative-level pulp collection infrastructure would create a new revenue stream while reducing waterway contamination. R_p 42 to 60+ (failure to stressed zone) Manufacturer SLB with packaging recyclability rate as performance trigger. Revenue basis: EPR levy avoidance and brand sustainability premium.
