Structural Bottleneck: Phase 1 — Fermentation and Drying (76.5% balance, below π/4 operational reference of 78.5%)

Fermentation and sun-drying at farm level develops approximately 85% of chocolate flavor precursors over 12-22 days. No standardized process, no temperature monitoring, no chain-of-custody documents, and no identity preservation exist at this phase. Child labor is most concentrated here. Sustainability certifications issued at cooperative membership level are structurally incapable of verifying what happens during fermentation. D=272, C=208: institutional opacity and absence of measurement infrastructure dominate. Phase 6 (Manufacturing/Retail) is a secondary weak point at 80.2% due to the 1:6 farm-to-retail price ratio with only USD 200/tonne Fairtrade premium.
