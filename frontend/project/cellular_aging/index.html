Primary bottleneck: Phase 6 (Cell Division and Output: senescence). Phases 0–4 operate in the Coherent zone (91–96% balance), reflecting well-characterized and well-functioning cellular machinery in mid-adult life. The structural break occurs at Phase 3 (Protein Quality Control, 79.0%), where proteasome activity declines 30–50% with age and autophagy is suppressed by chronic mTOR overactivation. This quality control failure allows defective proteins to accumulate, which propagates D-C imbalance forward through Phase 5 (Epigenetic Regulation, 76.2%) and crystallizes into senescent cell accumulation at Phase 6 (71.6%). Critically, senescent cells do not merely fail to divide: they actively emit SASP inflammatory signals that degrade Phase 7 balance in neighboring cells. Phase 3 is the intervention leverage point; Phase 6 is the damage propagation amplifier.
