♻️ Cellular Recycling and Clearance Chain

Measures how well each phase in the cellular aging supply chain recycles, clears, or neutralizes its waste products and defective components. The cellular return chain includes autophagy, mitophagy, proteasomal recycling, senescent cell clearance (senolysis), and NAD+ recycling. TVPCI-R scores are PROVISIONAL: derived from published age-related decline data in the absence of standardized whole-cell lifecycle assessment (LCA) equivalents for biological systems.

Tholonic Framework

TVPCI, TVPCI-R, and B_chain in Cellular Terms

TVPCI (D Role: Definition)
The primary cellular supply chain balance: how well each phase converts its inputs to useful outputs. Measures cellular productivity: ATP generated, proteins folded, DNA replicated, cells divided. Represents the definition of what the cell IS doing.
85.0
TVPCI-R (C Role: Contribution)
The cellular return chain balance: how well each phase recycles its waste, clears damaged components, and closes the loop. Autophagy, mitophagy, senolysis, NAD+ recycling, and DNA repair are all return chain processes. Represents what the cell GIVES BACK to maintain systemic health.
58.7
B_chain (N Role: Negotiation)
The holon balance between primary and return chains. B_chain = 100 × exp(−2 × |TVPCI − TVPCI_R| / max(TVPCI, TVPCI_R)). A score of 53.9 indicates that the return chain is critically lagging the primary chain. Aging biology encapsulated in a single number.
53.9: Failure
Chain Imbalance

TVPCI vs. TVPCI-R Delta

TVPCI (primary) vs. TVPCI-R (return): signed imbalance = −26.3 points
TVPCI: 85.0 TVPCI-R: 58.7
Δ = −26.3 (return chain critically lags primary chain)

This imbalance is the tholonic signature of biological aging: the cellular machinery continues to function (TVPCI = 85.0, Good zone) but its recycling and waste clearance systems degrade much faster (TVPCI-R = 58.7, Failure zone). The gap widens with age as Phase 3 proteasome decline, Phase 6 senescent cell accumulation, and Phase 1 mitophagy impairment compound each other.

Per-Phase Return Scores (PROVISIONAL)

R_p Scores: Recycling Performance by Phase

R_p measures how observable and accountable each phase's cellular recycling and waste clearance is. Omega weights (sum = 1.0) are proportional to waste volume and systemic impact. Zone thresholds: Coherent ≥ 80, Stressed 61.8–80, Failure 38.2–61.8, Breakdown < 38.2.

Mitophagy (PINK1/Parkin pathway) selectively clears damaged mitochondria. PINK1 and Parkin expression and activity decline with age, impairing this quality-gate. 74
Stressed
ω = 0.08 · Dominant waste: aborted translation products; misfolded nascent chains; deacylated tRNAs
Ribosome-associated quality control (RQC) system catches aborted translation products and directs them to Phase 3 for proteasomal degradation. RQC capacity decreases with age due to declining Listerin (LTN1) E3 ligase activity. This passes more defective nascent proteins to an already-stressed Phase 3 system.

Protein Quality Control
55
Failure
ω = 0.18 · Dominant waste: protein aggregates (tau, alpha-syn, TDP-43); ubiquitin chains; lysosomal contents
Phase 3 IS the recycling phase for the entire supply chain. Its R_p = 55 reflects the fundamental paradox: the system responsible for recycling everything else is itself the most severely degraded by age. Proteasome throughput halved; autophagy suppressed by mTOR. When this phase fails, aggregate waste propagates to every downstream phase. Highest priority circular intervention target.
4
DNA Replication Fidelity
67
Stressed
ω = 0.12 · Dominant waste: oxidatively damaged bases (8-OHdG); double-strand breaks; telomere fragments
Base excision repair (BER) and nucleotide excision repair (NER) recycle oxidatively damaged nucleotides. PARP1 (NAD+-dependent) coordinates DNA repair; NAD+ decline with age impairs this recycling. Telomere fragments shed from critically short telomeres activate cGAS-STING: another Phase 4 waste stream feeding Phase 7.

50
Failure
ω = 0.10 · Dominant waste: derepressed transposable elements (LINE-1 RNA); inappropriate gene products; mislocalized H3K27me3
Epigenetic recycling means the resetting of methylation patterns and histone marks to appropriate cell-type-specific targets. This is extremely low transparency: unlike protein aggregates or mtDNA heteroplasmy, epigenetic drift is difficult to measure at the individual cell level. LINE-1 retrotransposon RNA released from derepressed genomic elements activates innate immune sensors: another Phase 5 waste stream affecting Phase 7.

Cell Division & Output
43
Breakdown
ω = 0.18 · Dominant waste: senescent cells (SASP emitters); MMP3; IL-6; IL-8; GDF-15
Senescent cell clearance (senolysis) is the return chain for Phase 6. In young organisms, NK cells and macrophages efficiently clear senescent cells shortly after they form. In aged organisms, NK cytotoxicity declines ~40%, allowing senescent cells to persist indefinitely. This is the lowest R_p in the chain and the only phase in the Breakdown zone. Senolytic interventions (D+Q, fisetin, CAR-T) are the targeted return chain repair tools for this phase.

Intercellular Signaling
47
Failure
ω = 0.11 · Dominant waste: chronic cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha); circulating cell-free DNA; LPS fragments
Cytokine clearance and resolution of inflammatory signaling requires both anti-inflammatory resolution mediators (lipoxins, resolvins from omega-3) and immune-regulatory feedback (IL-10, TGF-beta). Both decline with age. Chronic inflammatory cytokines have no efficient recycling mechanism: they simply have to be blocked at production (Phase 6 senolysis) or their downstream effects mitigated by anti-inflammatory mediators.
