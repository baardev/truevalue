Counterfactual: What Early Intervention Would Have Recovered

If the N-D-C structural signal had been acted upon in Q3 2021 (the quarter Phase 3 crossed into Breakdown), the following resolution path was available:

  • Supervisory requirement to restore hedging coverage: Phase 3 balance recovery to 55-65%. No mark-to-market insolvency.
  • Required asset rebalancing: HTM reduced from 55% to 30% of assets over 4-6 quarters. Losses recognized gradually under AFS classification rather than catastrophically at failure.
  • Private capital raise at 2021 valuations: SVB market cap in 2021 was $25-35B. Capital raise under controlled conditions would have been successful. Failure capital raise in March 2023 failed because the structural collapse was already terminal.

Estimated counterfactual outcome: No FDIC receivership. FDIC fund not depleted (~$20B saved). Shareholders retain substantial value. Tech sector banking stability maintained. Systemic risk exception not required. Total economic value preserved: estimated $35-50B compared to actual loss of approximately $30-40B in aggregate (equity, franchise, fund, contagion costs).
