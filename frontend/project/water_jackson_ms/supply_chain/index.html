Jackson Water System Supply Chain

Broken-system case study for a 12-phase municipal water cycle. The model tracks source water, intake, filtration, treatment control, storage, distribution pressure, household delivery, wastewater, storm stress, public notice, and emergency restoration when the maintenance lifecycle fails.
