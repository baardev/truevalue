Why This Project Exists

This case represents a visibly broken common infrastructure system. It contrasts with high-functioning water models by showing what happens when a built potable water system is not sustained by maintenance, staffing, capital renewal, and emergency repair capacity.

Physical Chain

  • Source water and watershed condition
  • Raw water intake and pumping
  • Coagulation, sedimentation, and filtration
  • Disinfection and chemical treatment control
  • Clearwell, storage, and finished-water pumping
  • Distribution network pressure
  • Household delivery and access
  • Wastewater collection and conveyance
  • Wastewater treatment and discharge
  • Stormwater and flood stress interaction
  • Regulatory monitoring and public notice
  • Emergency water distribution and service restoration

N-D-C Interpretation

The defining failure is not source-water scarcity. It is imbalance between high definition constraints, including safe-drinking-water rules and pressure requirements, and weak contribution capacity, including treatment reliability, pump performance, pressure maintenance, repair execution, and public service delivery.

Source Categories

Recommended source categories include EPA emergency orders, DOJ consent decree materials, Mississippi State Department of Health notices, city operational reports, public infrastructure assessments, and outage or boil-water notice time series.
