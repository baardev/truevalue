Bottleneck: Phase 4 — Dutch Manufacturing (85.1%, Fair zone)

Phase 4 is structural rather than opacity-driven. Two coexisting business models create D-C imbalance: Tony's Chocolonely (no CBE, 100% traceable, USD 2,700/t farm premium target) versus conventional CBE-using manufacturers (Verkade/Mondelez, private-label). Retail buyer concentration (Albert Heijn ~35% grocery share, Jumbo ~22%) and EU PPWR packaging requirements drive D=268. C=228 reflects Dutch consumer sustainability leadership and Tony's model as industry benchmark. At 85.1%, this is in the Fair zone — far better than the global cocoa Phase 1 bottleneck at 76.5% (failure zone).
