Chain Minimum: Phase 4 — Dutch Manufacturing (85.1%, Coherent zone, above π/4 reference of 78.5%)

Phase 4 is structural rather than opacity-driven. Two coexisting business models create D-C imbalance: Tony's Chocolonely (no CBE, 100% traceable, USD 2,700/t farm premium target) versus conventional CBE-using manufacturers (Verkade/Mondelez, private-label). Retail buyer concentration (Albert Heijn ~35% grocery share, Jumbo ~22%) and EU PPWR packaging requirements drive D=268. C=228 reflects Dutch consumer sustainability leadership and Tony's model as industry benchmark. At 85.1%, this is above the π/4 operational reference (78.5%) and in the Coherent PHI zone. Compare: global cocoa Phase 1 at 76.5% falls below the π/4 reference, making it a true operational bottleneck.
