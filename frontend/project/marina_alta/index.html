Structural Alert: Triple Agricultural Crisis + Governance Vacuum

The Marina Alta ecosystem faces simultaneous collapse across all three anchor commodities. Cherry production fell 94% in Vall de Gallinera (800,000 kg to 40,000-50,000 kg) primarily because pollinator trees now flower at a different time than cultivator trees due to climate shift. Almond groves have been devastated by Xylella fastidiosa (no cure; hundreds of thousands of trees felled). Olive oil production is down from extended drought. The underlying aquifer water governance body (CASAMA) has been inactive since 1987. Phase 6 (Crop Transition, balance 75.0%) and Phase 2 (Harvest, balance 76.5%) are the critical intervention points.

Cherries (PGI Anchor)

In Crisis

Cerezas de la Montana de Alicante PGI. Production collapsed 94% in Vall de Gallinera. 2026 first recovery (3-4M kg, EUR 25M projected). Key threat: pollinator tree timing mismatch from climate shift. Response: artificial pollination, 2D orchard design.

🫒

Premium EVOO

Stressed

Native varietals: Blanqueta, Alfafarenca, Mancanella. Top producers: Masia el Altet (Flos Olei), Senorios de Relleu (oleotourism), Almazara La Alqueria (CrowdFarming), Finca Rontonar (Slow Food). Production down from drought.

🌰

Almonds

Stressed

Devastated by Xylella fastidiosa (no cure). 2025/26 surge to 16,500 T in-shell after good winter rains. New varieties: Guara and Soleta (self-pollinating, late-blooming). Revenue EUR 3-6/kg. Quicker ROI than pistachio.

🍃

Pistachios

Opportunity

Green gold. EUR 7-12/kg vs EUR 0.63-0.83/kg for bulk olives. Spain: 70,000+ ha national. Semi-arid inland (Alto Vinalopo, Alcoia). 5-7 year establishment. La Mancha farmers achieve 2,000 kg/ha.

🌱

Carob (Algarroba)

Opportunity

350mm rainfall minimum. Limestone soil-adapted. High demand in pharmacological, cosmetic, health food industries (chocolate substitute, locust bean gum). Viveros Hernandorena leading intensification programme.

💧

Aquifer Management

Critical Gap

Highest recharge rate in province (24%, 189mm/yr) but CASAMA water governance inactive since 1987. Marine intrusion active. Drip irrigation vs potable water trade-off. Solutions: reclaimed water, storage ponds, integrated governance.
