Marina Alta: Supply Chain Analysis

8-phase N-D-C chain from aquifer ecosystem baseline through harvest, processing, quality certification, market distribution, and ecosystem regeneration. Chain average balance: 86.3%. Bottleneck phases: Phase 2 (Harvest, 76.5%) and Phase 6 (Crop Transition, 75.0%). Structural cause: simultaneous triple crisis in cherries (pollination mismatch), almonds (Xylella), and EVOO (drought), compounded by the CASAMA aquifer governance vacuum since 1987.
