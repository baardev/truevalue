Bottleneck Alert: Phases 6 and 2: Investment Gap and Harvest Labour Crisis

Phase 6 (Crop Transition, balance 75.0%) has the highest D-value in the chain (D=260) because the 5-7 year establishment period for pistachio and carob requires patient capital that small mountain farms cannot self-finance. Phase 2 (Harvest, balance 76.5%) is broken by the intersection of three simultaneous failures: pollinator tree timing mismatch (cherry), Xylella fastidiosa (almond), and extreme weather sensitivity (4 days of fog = 50% crop loss). The D-C imbalance in Phase 6 (gap = 65) is the primary bond intervention target.
