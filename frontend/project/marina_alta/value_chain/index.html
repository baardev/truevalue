Investment scenario modelling for crop transition, technology adoption, and EU Horizon funding leverage. Financial data being assembled from the PPTX business plan (January 2024) and public market price sources.

Financial Abstraction Layer (e-score)

71.0
e-score: Financial abstraction coupling (PROVISIONAL / Stressed zone)

The e-score of 71.0 indicates that financial instruments (EU Horizon bonds, Generalitat grants, private investment) are available in principle but are not yet formally linked to the ecosystem performance metrics. The CASAMA water governance vacuum and the absence of a coordinated basin-scale monitoring programme prevent the e-layer from activating at full coherence.

To activate the full financial abstraction layer, three conditions must be met: (1) CASAMA reactivation or equivalent water governance structure established; (2) Systematic per-farm production and harvest data collection infrastructure in place; (3) Agricultural Climate Adaptation Bond instrument formally structured and piloted with at least one government or development finance institution as anchor investor.

Five-Model Value Context

Model AxisScoreValue Chain Interpretation
pi (π): Operational 86.3 Chain is operationally viable when weather permits. 2026 recovery season demonstrates inherent productive capacity. The platform exists; the disruptions are climate-driven, not structural to the market mechanism.
phi (φ): Proportional 82.0 Value distribution is reasonably proportional through the chain. Premium EVOO producers capture significant margin. Cherry PGI provides some price floor. However, the collapse of volume production prevents proportional scale benefits from materialising.
sqrt2 (√2): Structural 78.0 Terraced mountain terrain adds material structural overhead: no mechanised access to many cherry plots, hand-picking only, limited lorry access. 2D orchard design (UFO system) and drip irrigation infrastructure directly improve this score.
ln2: Growth 80.0 Value transformation is partially captured. Cherry PGI and EVOO certification create premium anchors. The Los Grobos scaling model (currently USD 500M+) demonstrates what growth looks like if the cherry cooperative model is professionalized. Pistachio transition unlocks a step-change in growth potential from Year 5.
e: Financial Abstraction 71.0 Financial instruments are available (EU Horizon EUR 83M; Generalitat grants; SLB framework) but are not yet formally linked to measurable ecosystem performance. CASAMA reactivation and systematic data collection are pre-conditions for upgrading the e-score. Target: 85+ once bond is structured and first KPI cycle complete.
