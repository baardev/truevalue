Program Overview

The PhD in Environmental and Planetary Health Sciences (EPHS) applies an interdisciplinary approach to identifying public health impacts of the local and global environment, human activities, the food environment and nutrition, and the socio-political and economic factors that determine sustainability. It is one of three doctoral concentrations at CUNY SPH, alongside Community Health and Health Policy and Epidemiology.

The program is designed for students who are passionate about promoting public health equity through a framework of environmental justice and sustainability stewardship. Graduates are expected to apply scientific methods to understand and address global challenges.

As of Fall 2024, the EPHS doctoral concentration enrolls 16 students, the smallest of the three doctoral programs (Community Health: 50; Epidemiology: 35). The 2025 incoming cohort consists of 4 students.

Program constraints (D-side) Fully documented Low impact: admissions, credit structure, and exam requirements are public and complete.
