E-Layer Coupling (BIC)
Primary Bottleneck — Phase 8: End-of-Life & Waste Management (56.7% balance): 10 billion lighters enter waste streams every year with <1% material recovery. The lighter body is technically recyclable PP but excluded from all curbside streams due to residual butane (hazardous waste classification). No producer take-back programme exists at scale. Estimated waste: 50,000 MT PP/yr + 14,500 MT CO2e from butane venting. France (CITEO REP) is the only national EPR regime covering lighter packaging — the device itself carries no global EPR obligation. D-C imbalance: 215 vs 85 (regulatory barriers without circularity infrastructure).

Secondary Bottlenecks — Phases 1 and 2 (70.0% and 72.0% balance): Component Manufacturing and Lighter Assembly are structurally opaque. Approximately 3,000 private factories in the Wenzhou/Zhejiang cluster produce all components and assemble 10 billion units per year with no public production data.
