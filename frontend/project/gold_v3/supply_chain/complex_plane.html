D-C Complex Plane

Each supply chain phase is represented as a complex number z = D + iC. The argument θ = arctan(C/D) encodes the same ratio information as the balance score. The modulus |z| = √(D² + C²) adds what the balance score cannot see: total operational load. Two phases with identical balance scores but different moduli have the same D/C health but different physical scale. This visualization is only meaningful because D and C are threshold-ratio normalized (gold_v2): scale reflects real operational intensity, not flag-count artifacts.

Key thresholds in the complex plane: The 45° diagonal is perfect balance (D = C, balance = 100%). The Coherent zone spans 37.3° to 52.7° (balance ≥ 61.8%, phi⁻¹ threshold). The Stressed zone spans 27.4°–37.3° and 52.7°–62.6° (balance 38.2%–61.8%). Below 27.4° or above 62.6° is the Failure zone. A perfectly balanced phase at the viability threshold (D = C = 1) has modulus = √2 = 1.414, the structural constant of the cTVF.

Interpretation

Modulus as operational load

Phase 6 (Vaulting) has the highest modulus (2.789) — it carries the heaviest combined constraint-plus-contribution load in the chain. Phase 7 (Exchange) has the lowest (1.544) — it is the lightest, closest to its viability minimum on both sides. Modulus and balance zone are independent: a phase can be coherent but heavy (Phase 5, Bar Casting: |z| = 2.391) or stressed but not extremely heavy (Phase 6: |z| = 2.789).

Argument as health indicator

All phases except Phase 6 fall between 42° and 47°, tightly clustered near the ideal 45° line. Phase 6 is at 37.66°, just inside the Stressed zone boundary (37.32°). This shows the gold supply chain is structurally well-balanced in ratio terms: the flag-count model's Coherent results are confirmed by the physically grounded threshold-ratio model.

The √2 reference point

A perfectly balanced phase at the exact minimum viable threshold (D = C = 1) sits at the point (1, 1) with modulus = √2 = 1.414. This is the cTVF structural constant emerging geometrically from the complex plane. Phases closer to this point are simpler, lighter transitions; phases farther out are heavier but not necessarily less healthy.

What the balance score alone misses

Phases 1 (Mining) and 7 (Exchange) have similar balance scores (91.6% and 93.2%). Their moduli are very different: 1.852 vs 1.544. Mining carries 20% more combined operational load than Exchange. The balance score treats them as nearly equivalent; the complex plane reveals that Mining is substantially heavier. This distinction matters for resource allocation and intervention design.
