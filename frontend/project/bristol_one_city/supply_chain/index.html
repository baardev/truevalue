N-D-C Framework Applied to Urban Policy Delivery

In the Tholonic N-D-C framework, D (Definition) captures the constraints, requirements, governance obligations, and binding commitments that define what each delivery phase must achieve. C (Contribution) captures the actual outputs, measurable delivery, and flows produced by each phase. N (Negotiation) is the coherent emergent state of the phase: the product of D and C in balance.

For Bristol, D across Phases 0-2 is very high: the city has invested heavily in policy commitments, governance structures, and binding strategies. The D-C gap widens at Phase 3 (capital) and collapses into a structural failure at Phase 5 (community uptake). Phase 6 (measurement) recovers because Bristol's monitoring infrastructure is genuinely world-class: the QoL survey (256 indicators), BACCC independent scrutiny, and City Leap contractual audit form an unusually robust verification layer. This measurement strength is the foundation on which a Sustainability-Linked Bond KPI framework can be built.
