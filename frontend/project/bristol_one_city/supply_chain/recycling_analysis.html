Framework: TVPCI, TVPCI-R, and B_chain

In the Tholonic framework, the primary delivery chain (TVPCI) measures D-C balance: how well the institutional architecture (D) is matched by actual delivery (C). The return chain (TVPCI-R) measures a different question: of the value generated by the delivery chain, how much circulates back to the communities that most need it? For Bristol, this is the "just transition" dimension: the degree to which the decarbonisation transition also reduces inequality and improves outcomes for the 53,100 residents in the most deprived 10% nationally.

Per-Phase Return Scores (R_p) and Omega Weights

All R_p scores and omega weights are PROVISIONAL. R_p measures how observable and accountable each phase's community return is. Omega weights are proportional to community impact potential at each phase and must sum to 1.0. Zone thresholds: Coherent R_p ≥ 80; Stressed 61.8-79; Failure 38.2-61.8; Breakdown < 38.2.

Community Impact Assessment: Who Receives the Return?

The omega weights above reflect community impact potential: Phase 5 carries the highest weight (0.25) because it is the direct community interface, and Phase 4 (0.22) because infrastructure physically enters deprived communities. The collapse of R_p at Phase 5 (35, Breakdown) combined with its high omega weight is the primary driver of the low TVPCI-R.

Priority Just Transition Interventions (Bond-Linked)

The following interventions are the highest-priority actions to close the TVPCI-TVPCI-R gap. Each is structured to be measurable, phase-specific, and bondable (i.e. capable of serving as a KPI or SPT in a Sustainability-Linked Bond or Local Climate Bond tranche).

PhaseInterventionDescriptionExpected R_p impactBond/finance link
P5 (primary) Net-Zero Neighbourhood demonstrators in 3 deprived wards Co-design and fund net-zero retrofit of c.1,000 properties each in Hartcliffe/Withywood, Filwood, and Lawrence Hill (Bristol's three most deprived wards). Blended finance: SLB (senior) + City Funds (mezzanine) + pay-as-you-save (junior). Living Places Earth model. R_p P5: 35 → approximately 55-60 (+20-25 pts). Omega = 0.25. TVPCI-R impact: +5-6 pts overall. SLB SPT: number of properties retrofitted in IMD top-10% wards. Observation date: 2027 (1,000 props), 2030 (3,000 props).
P3 (capital) Deprived-ward BCAI tranche with council co-contribution Third BCAI tranche at £1 minimum entry, ISA-eligible, with a council match-contribution for Universal Credit recipients. Target: 10,000 investors from the 53,100 in the most deprived decile. Raise BCAI total to £5-7m. R_p P3: 48 → approximately 58-62 (+10-14 pts). Omega = 0.18. TVPCI-R impact: +2-3 pts. SLB SPT: number of BCAI investors from IMD top-10% postcode areas. Verification: Abundance Investment investor postcode data.
P5 and P6 Scope 3 ward-level consumption tracking Partner with Cabot Institute to add a consumption-based emissions supplement to the annual QoL Survey. Create ward-level scope 3 tracking for 34 wards, disaggregated by deprivation decile. Establishes the data layer needed for scope 3 SPTs in a future SLB. R_p P5: +8-10 pts (new measurable output). R_p P6: +10-12 pts (data quality improvement). Combined TVPCI-R impact: +3-4 pts. Pre-condition for scope 3 SLB KPI. Bond-linkable once methodology established and first data published.
P3 and P5 SLB issuance with life expectancy gap SPT Issue a £50-100m Sustainability-Linked Bond with the male life expectancy gap (most vs least deprived wards) as a Social SPT alongside the environmental KPIs. 25 bps step-up if gap exceeds 10.0 years at 2030 observation. TrueValue N-D-C scoring provides annual independent verification of all SPTs. R_p P3: +15 pts (institutional capital with equity accountability). TVPCI-R systemic impact: +4-6 pts when all KPIs are linked to community return. B_chain: 47.0 → approximately 58-62. Direct SLB SPT: male life expectancy gap at 2027 and 2030 observation dates. Coupon step-up of 25 bps if missed. Verification: PHOF (A02a), ONS data.

Calculation Reference

R_p = community return score for phase p (0-100): how observable and accountable the phase's community return is. Omega_p = phase weight (proportional to community impact potential; sums to 1.0). TVPCI_R = Σ(omega_p × R_p). B_chain = 100 × exp(-2 × |TVPCI - TVPCI-R| / max(TVPCI, TVPCI-R)). Zone thresholds (phi-derived): Coherent R_p ≥ 80 | Stressed 61.8-79 | Failure 38.2-61.8 | Breakdown < 38.2.
