Value Chain Analysis: Deferred

The Bristol One City value chain centres on the structure of a Sustainability-Linked Bond (SLB) that would translate the One City delivery chain into an institutional-capital instrument. This layer is deferred pending completion of three prerequisites listed below.

When activated, this layer will include: SLB KPI selection (5 axes: E, S, G, mapped to One City missions); Sustainability Performance Target (SPT) calibration against City Leap contractual KPIs and QoL Survey baselines; bond characteristic design (size, tenor, coupon step-up triggers at 2027 and 2030 observation dates); ICMA SLBP Second Party Opinion requirements; and an annual TrueValue N-D-C verification framework mapped to the 7-phase delivery chain.

The e-axis coherence score of 52.0 (the lowest in the five-model framework) confirms that this layer is the critical gap in the Bristol system. The supply chain and return chain analyses provide the quantitative foundation; this layer converts that foundation into an investable financial product.

Prerequisites for Value Chain Activation

  • Phase 5 neighbourhood demonstrator data: at least one Net-Zero Neighbourhood pilot operational with measurable household uptake data (R_p and omega calibration)
  • Scope 3 tracking methodology established: ward-level consumption emissions data published (first QoL Survey supplement complete)
  • Institutional investor engagement: at least one pension fund or insurance company investor expressing interest in Bristol SLB at indicative terms, confirming market appetite for the SPT structure
  • ICMA SLBP external review (Second Party Opinion) commissioned: KPI materiality and SPT ambition level independently confirmed
