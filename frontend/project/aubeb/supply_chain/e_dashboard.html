⚠️ Data Note — e-Model Opacity and Estimation

Physical and abstract values are drawn from the AUBEB PDI baseline, programme-scale assumptions, and synthetic scenario records. Coupling ratios marked ESTIMATED are derived from available evidence but are not directly disclosed. Site-level disbursement and benefit-sharing records are marked OPAQUE until the programme publishes auditable records. The opacity itself is the analytical finding: a phase where the parent-to-child N transition cannot be traced represents a structural break in the tholonic hierarchy.

What the e Score Reveals About Mangrove

The e model uses D=1, C=1, the identity case of the tholonic triadic structure. In a perfectly coherent system, every abstract credit, certification, finance, or benefit claim corresponds to verified hectares, carbon stocks, restoration work, or community benefit records. The mathematical constant e emerges from continuous compounding: e = lim(n→∞)(1 + 1/n)ⁿ. This is the dynamic at work when abstract claims compound faster than the verified physical restoration evidence beneath them.

The current system e-coherence score of 37 means AUBEB's abstract layer operates at roughly 37% coupling efficiency with its verified physical and ecological substrate. The dominant failure modes are certification and credit claims that need registry confirmation, fund deployment that needs site-level disbursement traceability, and community benefit claims that need local governance records.

The e model makes the invisible visible: it draws a boundary between the physical restoration chain and the abstract claims layer that has grown around it. The physical chain restores and maintains hectares of mangrove ecosystem and verifies carbon and livelihood outcomes. The abstract layer, credits, certificates, bond proceeds, and benefit entitlements, must remain tethered to those verified records.
