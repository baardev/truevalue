Programme Overview

The African Union Blue Economy Bond (AUBEB) is a large-scale coastal ecosystem restoration programme targeting 150,000 ha of mangrove forest across seven AU member states. The programme is led by the African Union Commission (AUC) in partnership with Global Underwater Diagnostics (GUD), using the ODIN dMRV platform for satellite-based measurement, reporting, and verification.


The supply chain spans 11 phases (Phase 0–10), from pre-commercial degraded ecosystem baseline through active Community-Based Ecological Mangrove Restoration (CBEMR) to a verified, sustained blue-economy asset. The programme monetises through voluntary carbon markets (VCM), CORSIA-compliant credits, fisheries benefit shares, and an AUBEB-labelled bond structured for institutional investors.


Material class: Ecosystem services (blue carbon)  |  Standard: Flexible (Verra / Gold Standard / Plan Vivo / ICR / ACR)  |  Article 6.2 authorisation: Anticipated  |  Crediting period: 30 years  |  Programme scale: USD 84.6M+ annual capacity

Phase Intervention Worksheet

PROVISIONAL: Recommendations are derived analytically from PDI phase-level N-D-C data and public sources. Field validation is required before capital commitment. Each intervention is grounded in the specific mathematical axis that identifies the type of failure.

How the Five-Model Constants Diagnose Failure: AUBEB Blue Economy Restoration Chain

Each coherence axis (pi, phi, sqrt2, ln2, e) measures a different dimension of systemic health. The N-D-C balance formula is balance = 100 - (|D - C| / max(D, C)) * 100. A failing phase has D significantly greater than C: the constraint load exceeds contribution capacity. Chain average balance: 87.6%.
