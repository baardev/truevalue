The structural price and margin data from primary sources (CaixaBank Research 2022, Olive Oil Times, Persistence Market Research 2026) are incorporated in the tables below. Full transaction-level value chain metrics (farmgate vs. cooperative vs. processor vs. retailer margin by lot) are not yet available and would require direct cooperation with Spanish cooperatives (Dcoop, Hojiblanca) or the Spanish Ministry of Agriculture value chain study (MAPA 2021). The analysis below reflects the structural findings derivable from public sources.

62.0
e-Layer Score (Financial Abstraction Coupling)
The olive oil value chain operates primarily as a physical commodity with limited financial abstraction. No dedicated olive oil futures contract (unlike gold or grain) exists at a major exchange. Cooperative hedging is limited. The score of 62.0 reflects that price signals do flow from retail to farmgate but with significant lag and information asymmetry. Activating the full financial abstraction layer would require formal futures market participation (CME or EU equivalent) for Spanish olive oil and financial instrument integration by Dcoop/Hojiblanca.

Five-Model Value Context

ModelScoreValue Chain Implication
π — Operational89.1Physical production and logistics are efficient. The supply chain problem is not operational; it is at the brand/market interface.
φ — Proportional74.5Value distribution is significantly below sustainable threshold. EUR 1.20/kg gap means Spain captures only ~73% of achievable unit value. This is the core value chain failure requiring intervention.
√2 — Structural82.0Supply chain structure (cooperatives, logistics) is sound. Italian relabeling introduces a structural opacity layer that deprives Spain of traceability-based premium.
ln2 — Growth71.5Value transformation rate is low: Spain inputs raw material; Italy applies transformation (brand) and captures value. Growth investment must flow to Phase 5 (brand) and Phase 7 (market access) to correct this.
e — Financial62.0No formal olive oil futures market. Cooperative hedging limited. Commodity pricing dominates producer economics. A structured bond linked to brand KPIs would create the first financial abstraction layer for the sector.
