Why This Project Exists

This case represents a common infrastructure system that became dysfunctional under stress. Unlike a normal supply chain, an electric grid must balance supply and load continuously. When fuel, generation, balancing, and distribution all degrade together, the failure propagates immediately.

Physical Chain

  • Weather forecast and demand expectation
  • Natural gas production and fuel supply
  • Fuel transport and gas-electric dependency
  • Generation winterization and plant readiness
  • Generation dispatch and fleet availability
  • Transmission and interconnection limits
  • Control room frequency balancing
  • Load shedding and emergency operations
  • Distribution feeder outage management
  • Critical services and dependent infrastructure
  • Customer outage exposure and public communication
  • Emergency restoration and black-start readiness

N-D-C Interpretation

The grid had strong definition constraints: frequency stability, reserve requirements, cold-weather demand, and critical-load obligations. Contribution capacity collapsed when fuel supply and generation availability failed, forcing emergency load shedding to preserve the remaining system N-state.
