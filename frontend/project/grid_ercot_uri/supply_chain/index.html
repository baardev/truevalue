ERCOT Winter Storm Uri Grid Supply Chain

Broken-system case study for a 12-phase real-time electrical grid cascade. The model follows forecast, fuel supply, gas-electric dependency, winterization, generation dispatch, transmission, balancing, load shedding, distribution, critical services, customer exposure, and emergency restoration during a cold-weather event.
