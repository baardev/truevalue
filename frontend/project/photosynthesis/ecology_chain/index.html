Ecological Chain Analytical Layers — Three Tabs in the Dashboard

All three layers use the same 8 C3 phases. The dashboard at the link above provides a three-tab interactive view. Switching tabs changes the analytical lens without changing the underlying phase structure or data.

Tab 1: Ecological Outputs
Carbon fixation rates, O2 evolution, biomass production, and carbon sequestration potential per phase. The primary ecological performance metrics of the system.
Tab 2: Energy Efficiency
Quantum yield, ATP/NADPH production, thermodynamic losses, and stoichiometric efficiency at each phase. Compares actual (6%) vs theoretical (11%) whole-chain efficiency.
Tab 3: Stress Responses
D-C imbalance under heat, drought, high light, and elevated CO2 stress. Shows which phases are stressed under each condition, direction of imbalance, and severity.
For alternative analytical layers (e.g. economic valuation, carbon credit coupling), the e-model axis (currently unmeasured, score 0) would activate once financial abstraction data is added to the ecological output chain. See hub page for the five-model coherence signature.
