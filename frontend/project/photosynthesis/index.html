Primary bottleneck: Phase 5 (Rubisco carbon fixation). All eight light reaction phases (0-4) operate with near-perfect D-C balance (coherent zone, >94%). The structural imbalance emerges at Phase 5, where Rubisco must compete with O2 for the same active site. Under current atmospheric conditions (~21% O2), approximately 25% of light energy captured at Phases 0-4 is consumed by photorespiration rather than net carbon fixation. This is not an engineering failure; it is the evolutionary cost of a bifunctional enzyme that predates the oxygenation of Earth's atmosphere. The D-C gap at Phase 5 (D = 270, C = 230, balance = 85.2%) is the defining structural feature of C3 photosynthesis and the primary selection pressure that drove the evolution of C4 and CAM pathways.

Pathway Analysis Scope

C3 Active — C4 and CAM Available as Future Instances

This project analyzes canonical C3 photosynthesis as the reference pathway because it is the most studied, data-rich, and structurally complete case. The same N-D-C framework can be initialized for C4 and CAM from the BPDI form. The structural differences are substantial, not cosmetic.

Active
C3 Photosynthesis
Reference organisms: Spinacia oleracea, Arabidopsis thaliana, Triticum aestivum. 8 phases from LHCII to phloem loading. All eight phases directly mapped. Photorespiration at Phase 5 is the defining structural bottleneck. Accounts for ~85% of land plant species.
Initializable via BPDI
C4 Photosynthesis
Reference organisms: Zea mays (maize), Saccharum officinarum (sugarcane), Sorghum bicolor. Adds a Phase 5a (PEP carboxylase pre-fixation in mesophyll + bundle sheath transport). Phases 5-7 reorganized around the CO2-concentrating mechanism. Photorespiration suppressed (Vo/Vc ~0.02 vs 0.25 in C3). Higher efficiency under high temperature and light, lower under cool conditions.
Future instance
CAM Photosynthesis
Reference organisms: Agave tequilana, Opuntia ficus-indica (cactus), Ananas comosus (pineapple). Same 8 phases but temporally separated: stomata open at night for CO2 fixation into malate (Phases 5-7 at night); light reactions drive Calvin cycle during the day with stomata closed. Rate constants, phase timing, and stoichiometries differ completely from C3. Extreme water-use efficiency; low productivity.
To initialize a C4 or CAM instance: open the BPDI (Biological Phase Discovery Instrument) and select the desired pathway in the header. The form pre-loads the appropriate phase map.
Five-Model N·D·C Coherence

Photosynthesis — Five-Model Coherence Signature

Each vertex represents a Tholonic model derived from a mathematical constant. The outer zone (above 61.8%) is coherent (D:C ratio below phi); the middle zone (38.2–61.8%) is the failure zone (D:C between phi and phi²); the inner zone (below 38.2%) indicates breakdown. Thresholds are phi-derived: 61.8% = 1/phi, 38.2% = 1 − 1/phi. Note: the e (Abstract) axis is unmeasured (score 0) because photosynthesis has no financial abstraction layer. A score of 0 means unmeasured, not failed.
