Phase 5 Balance
Bottleneck: Phase 5 (Calvin Cycle: Carbon Fixation). D = 270, C = 230. The D-C gap is caused by Rubisco's bifunctionality: it fixes both CO2 (productive) and O2 (wasteful photorespiration). Under current atmosphere (21% O2), ~25% of light energy captured by Phases 0-4 is consumed by photorespiration. The chain average balance (95.4%) is high because all seven other phases are coherent; Phase 5 alone creates the structural deviation.
