Reading the φ Model

This model measures whether N-values at adjacent phases scale in golden-ratio (φ = 1.618) proportions. A ratio near 1.0 means adjacent phases have equal N-values (balanced but not escalating). A ratio near φ means each phase contributes proportionally more than the last.

0.85–1.20 Near-equal (phases matched in N-value)
1.20–1.80 Proportional (near golden-ratio escalation)
<0.85 or >1.80 Disproportionate (sharp step-up or step-down)

In photosynthesis, the N-values are tightly clustered between 213 (Phase 5) and 248 (Phase 3), so most ratios are near 1.0. The system is not designed for escalating value capture across phases; it is designed for maximum throughput coherence. The exception is the Phase 4→5 step-down (N drops from 232 to 213) caused by the Rubisco bottleneck.

Phase Boundary Proportional Analysis

Proportional Coherence Interpretation (Score: 74.2)

The photosynthesis φ score of 74.2 (stressed zone, 40-70% is failure, 70-100% is stressed-to-coherent) reflects the fact that N-values across the eight phases do not follow a monotonically increasing or golden-ratio-scaled pattern. This is expected: the photosynthetic chain is not an economic value chain where each step should amplify value. It is an energy conversion chain where phase outputs must be closely matched to phase inputs.


The most significant proportional deviation is at the Phase 4→5 boundary, where N drops from 232 (ATP synthase) to 213 (Rubisco fixation). This is the φ signature of the photorespiration bottleneck: the Rubisco phase cannot sustain the N-value established by the efficient light reactions because O2 competition reduces its effective contribution (C) relative to its constraints (D).


Comparison with gold (φ score 49.5): gold has a lower φ score because economic value extraction at phase boundaries is highly disproportionate (mining margins vs refining margins vs exchange margins). Photosynthesis maintains better proportional coherence because evolution has optimized for throughput, not for differential value extraction at any one phase.
