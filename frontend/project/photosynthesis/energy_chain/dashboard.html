Bottleneck detected: Phase 5 (Calvin Cycle: Carbon Fixation). Balance 85.2% (stressed zone). D = 270, C = 230. The D-C gap is structural: Rubisco cannot discriminate perfectly between CO2 and O2 as substrates. Under present atmosphere (~21% O2, ~0.04% CO2), the oxygenation/carboxylation ratio (Vo/Vc) = 0.25, meaning 25% of RuBP is consumed by photorespiration. All other phases are coherent (balance above 94%). The 95.4% chain average is high because the seven coherent phases dominate.
