ln(2) Model Applied to Photosynthesis

The ln(2) model measures whether transformation phases double the value of their inputs (C/D target = 2.0). In gold this captures whether institutional constraints suppress natural value doubling. In photosynthesis, the analogous question is: does the Calvin cycle double the energetic value of its ATP/NADPH inputs into fixed carbon?

The answer in C3 under ambient conditions is: no. The C/D ratio at Phase 5 is only 0.85 (C=230, D=270), far below the target 2.0. This is not an institutional constraint; it is a natural one (Rubisco bifunctionality). The ln(2) score of 65.5 reflects this: the Calvin cycle transformation phases (5-7) are operating below the natural doubling efficiency due to the photorespiratory energy drain.
