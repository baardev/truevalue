Bottleneck Alert: Phases 2 and 3: Interconnection Queues and Storage Shortfall

Phase 2 (Transmission, balance 74.1%) has the highest D-value in the chain (D=290) because interconnection queue backlogs, permitting delays, and cross-border transmission capacity shortfalls documented by IEA Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions analysis are constraints not yet matched by built transmission capacity (C=215). Phase 3 (Balancing and Storage, balance 74.5%) is broken by the same structural pattern: reserve margin and balancing requirements (D=275) outpace deployed storage and demand-response capacity (C=205). Curtailment of solar and wind, published by CAISO, ERCOT, and National Grid ESO, is the visible symptom of this gap. These are the primary bond intervention targets.
