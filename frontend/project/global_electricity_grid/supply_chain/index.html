Global Electricity Grid: Supply Chain Analysis

7-phase N-D-C chain from fuel and resource input through generation, transmission, balancing, distribution, end use, and retirement/decommissioning. BAU chain average balance: 81.8%. Bottleneck phases: Phase 2 (Transmission, 74.1%) and Phase 3 (Balancing and Storage, 74.5%). Structural cause: interconnection queue backlogs and permitting delays (Phase 2) combined with insufficient storage and demand-response buildout (Phase 3), both documented in IEA Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions analysis. The IEA NZE 2050-aligned mitigation scenario raises chain balance to 87.5% by targeting these two phases alone.
