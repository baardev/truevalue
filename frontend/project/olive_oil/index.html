Structural Bottleneck: Brand Capture Failure at Phase 5 (Packaging/Bottling)

Spain is the world's dominant olive oil producer but captures only EUR 3.22/kg average export price because ~67% of exports leave as unlabeled bulk. Italian companies (Salov/Filippo Berio, Deoleo/Bertolli, Monini) blend this Spanish oil into products sold under Italian-sounding brand names, realizing EUR 4.42/kg. On 742,500 tonnes exported in 2023/24, this EUR 1.20/kg gap represents approximately EUR 890 million in foregone annual revenue for Spanish producers and cooperatives. Phase 5 balance: 81.1%. Phase 7 (Trade/Markets): 85.5%.
