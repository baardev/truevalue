Structural Alert: C-Type Biogeochemical Flows Boundary Transgression

The chain-average balance of 82.6% is deceptively high. It masks a severe tail-end structural failure at Phase 5 (Environmental Return, balance 57.6%), the only phase in this chain that falls below the 61.8% stability threshold. Roughly 45% of nitrogen applied to cropland is lost to the environment as ammonia volatilization, nitrate runoff and leaching (causing eutrophication and algal blooms), and nitrous oxide emission, rather than taken up by crops. This is a C-heavy imbalance: uncontrolled output flow (C = 330) vastly exceeding the weak constraint layer (D = 190) at the field-to-environment interface. Per this repo's Paper 19, this is a textbook C-type Planetary Boundaries transgression (biosphere integrity, biogeochemical flows, ocean acidification, aerosols: violated by uncontrolled output), structurally distinct from a D-type transgression (climate, land-system change, freshwater, ozone, novel entities: violated by weak constraint). The biogeochemical flows boundary is one of the nine Rockstrom/Steffen/Richardson Planetary Boundaries and is documented as transgressed (Richardson et al. 2023, Science Advances). Phase 4 (Field Application, 67.7%, Stressed zone) is the secondary bottleneck, sharing the same C-heavy structural signature one step upstream.
