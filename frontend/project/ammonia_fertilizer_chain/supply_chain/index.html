Ammonia and Nitrogen Fertilizer Chain: Supply Chain Analysis

6-phase N-D-C chain from natural gas or coal feedstock through Haber-Bosch synthesis, downstream conversion into urea, ammonium nitrate and nitric acid, distribution, field application, and environmental return. Chain average balance: 82.6%. Bottleneck phases: Phase 5 (Environmental Return, 57.6%, the only Failure-zone phase) and Phase 4 (Field Application, 67.7%, Stressed). Structural cause: global cropland nitrogen-use efficiency of only about 55%, a C-type biogeochemical-flows Planetary Boundaries transgression per this repo's Paper 19.
