Bottleneck Alert: Phase 5 and Phase 4: C-Type Biogeochemical Flows Failure

Phase 5 (Environmental Return, balance 57.6%) is the only phase in this chain below the 61.8% stability threshold. Its D-C gap (D=190, C=330, gap=140) is the largest in the chain: nitrogen loss flows (ammonia volatilization, nitrate runoff and leaching, nitrous oxide emission) vastly exceed the weak constraint layer that would otherwise bound them at the field-to-environment interface. Phase 4 (Field Application, balance 67.7%, D=210, C=310, gap=100) is the secondary bottleneck, sharing the same C-heavy signature one step upstream: application volume is largely uncontrolled at farm level. Global cropland nitrogen-use efficiency is only about 55% (2020), meaning roughly 45% of applied nitrogen is lost rather than taken up by crops. This is a textbook C-type Planetary Boundaries transgression (biogeochemical flows) per this repo's Paper 19, distinct from a D-type constraint failure.
