BAU Bottleneck Alert: Phases 5-6 (Packaging, Certification)

Under BAU, shea butter never reaches international export channels. Phases 5-6 have near-zero N coherence (0.48 and 0.50) reflecting complete absence of export preparation and certification. This is the structural break point where almost all value leaks out of Burkina Faso. The Serious Shea model directly targets this gap with export infrastructure, international buyer contracts, and REDD+/organic certification pathways.
