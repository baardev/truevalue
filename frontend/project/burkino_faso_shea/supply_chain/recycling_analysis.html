TVPCI vs TVPCI-R — BAU and Serious Shea

TVPCI-R measures the extent to which waste streams generated within the supply chain are captured and returned as useful inputs (energy, nutrients, water). BAU discards nearly all by-products. Serious Shea closes the loop via biogas, syngas, compost, and water recycling.

Priority Circular Economy Interventions

Priority 1 — Immediate

Biogas from Shea Pulp

Install biodigester at each processing centre to convert shea fruit pulp into biogas. Primary energy input replacing fuelwood entirely for kernel boiling and butter processing heat.

Impact: Eliminates ~25 kgCO2e/kg butter GHG; fuelwood cost to zero; 320,000 tCO2e/yr avoided (10 centres)
Priority 2 — Immediate

Syngas from Nut Shells

Gasification of shea nut shells at processing centre to generate syngas for electricity generation. Displaces diesel generators and grid power demand.

Impact: Estimated 40-50% of processing electricity needs from shell gasification; reduces operating cost by ~30%
Priority 3 — Phase 2

Shea Cake Composting + Soil Return

Systematic composting of shea cake (protein-rich by-product of butter separation) with shea pulp residue. Apply as organic fertilizer to shea parklands.

Impact: Closes nutrient loop; improves parkland soil; increases shea tree yields 10-20% over 5-10 yrs; potential soil carbon credit
Priority 4 — Phase 2

Process Water Closed-Loop Recycling

Install filtration and recirculation system for kneading process water. Reduces freshwater draw by estimated 80%+ in each eco-processing centre.

Impact: 30-50 L/kg butter reduced to ~5-8 L/kg; critical for operations in BF's water-stressed Sahel regions
Priority 5 — Carbon Registry

REDD+ and Voluntary Carbon Credits

Register avoided emissions from fuelwood substitution plus shea parkland protection under World Bank REDD+ framework and voluntary carbon market.

Impact: Est. USD 1.6M-4.8M/yr carbon revenue at scale (320,000 tCO2e/yr × USD 5-15/t VCM price)
Priority 6 — Long-term

Shea Cake as Animal Feed / Aquaculture

Explore defatted shea cake (~50% protein) as a livestock or aquaculture feed supplement, extending value beyond compost use and creating additional local protein supply.

Impact: Additional revenue stream USD 50-150/t cake; reduces animal feed imports; rural economic diversification
