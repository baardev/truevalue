Solar PV: Value Chain Analysis

Policy and trade instrument landscape governing the geographic value-capture mismatch: China holds over 80% of manufacturing value across every stage while accounting for only 36% of global demand. Financial data assembled from IEA public reporting and published policy texts (IRA, EU Net Zero Industry Act, UFLPA, anti-dumping determinations).

Financial Abstraction Layer (e-score)

55.0
e-score: Financial abstraction coupling (PROVISIONAL / Stressed zone)

The e-score of 55.0 indicates that financial instruments (IRA 45X manufacturing credits, EU Net Zero Industry Act benchmarks, anti-dumping duties) exist and are actively enforced, but are not yet formally linked to phase-level physical diversification outcomes. Tariffs raise the cost of concentrated supply without directly financing diversified alternatives; manufacturing credits finance new capacity without a verified linkage to reduced single-facility concentration risk. This gap between financial instrument and physical outcome is the structural basis for the below-80 e-axis score.

To activate the full financial abstraction layer, three conditions would need to be met: (1) IRA 45X and EU NZIA credits explicitly conditioned on measured reductions in single-country or single-facility concentration ratios, not just on domestic production volume; (2) A standing, publicly reported facility-level capacity and ownership disclosure system across major producing and importing markets; (3) A sustainability-linked bond or credit instrument with KPIs tied directly to the phase-level D/C balance metrics in this analysis (concentration ratio, traceability coverage, take-back collection rate).
