Structural Alert: Extreme Geographic Concentration and Value Capture Mismatch

China's manufacturing share exceeds 80% at every one of the five production stages (polysilicon, ingots, wafers, cells, modules), more than double its 36% share of global PV deployment demand. This is the sharpest phi-axis (proportional value distribution) failure of any supply chain in this project's corpus, scoring 60.0, close to the 61.8% Failure boundary. Phase 1 (Ingot and Wafer Manufacturing, balance 65.0%) is the worst phase in the chain: China holds approximately 95-98% of global capacity, and one out of every seven solar panels produced worldwide comes from a single facility. Phase 0 (Polysilicon Production, balance 67.7%) carries a structural opacity and compliance gap: Xinjiang province alone accounts for about 40% of global polysilicon capacity, and UFLPA forced-labor import restrictions have targeted Xinjiang-sourced material since 2021-2022. Phase 5 (End-of-Life and Recycling, balance 73.1%) is a secondary stressed phase: under 10% of retired panels are formally recycled in most markets.

Policy and Trade Instrument Context

US IRA 45X
Advanced Manufacturing Production Credit: per-unit tax credits for domestic polysilicon, wafer, cell, and module production, targeting geographic diversification away from China concentration.
EU Net Zero Industry Act
Sets a 40% domestic manufacturing benchmark across strategic net-zero technologies, including solar PV, by 2030.
UFLPA (US)
Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act: import restrictions targeting Xinjiang-sourced polysilicon since 2021-2022 due to documented forced-labor concerns.
