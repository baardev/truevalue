Solar PV: Supply Chain Analysis

6-phase N-D-C chain from polysilicon production through ingot and wafer manufacturing, cell manufacturing, module assembly, installation and grid connection, and end-of-life recycling. Chain average balance: 79.0%. Bottleneck phases: Phase 1 (Ingot and Wafer Manufacturing, 65.0%) and Phase 0 (Polysilicon Production, 67.7%). Structural cause: China's manufacturing share exceeds 80% at every stage, most extreme at ingots and wafers (95-98%), driven by single-facility concentration and, in polysilicon, the Xinjiang forced-labor sourcing opacity gap under UFLPA.
