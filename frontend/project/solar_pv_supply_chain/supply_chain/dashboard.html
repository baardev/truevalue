Bottleneck Alert: Phases 1 and 0: Manufacturing Concentration and Sourcing Opacity

Phase 1 (Ingot and Wafer Manufacturing, balance 65.0%) has the highest D-value in the chain (D=300) because China's approximately 95-98% capacity share (up from 80% in 2010) has produced extreme single-point concentration: one out of every seven solar panels worldwide comes from a single facility. Phase 0 (Polysilicon Production, balance 67.7%, D=310, the highest D in the entire chain) is broken by the combination of feedstock purity requirements and the structural opacity created by UFLPA forced-labor import restrictions on Xinjiang-sourced material (approximately 40% of global polysilicon capacity) since 2021-2022. The D-C imbalance in Phase 0 (gap = 100) and Phase 1 (gap = 105) are the primary bond and policy intervention targets.
