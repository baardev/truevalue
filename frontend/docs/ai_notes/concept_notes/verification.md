# Verification

## Definition

Verification is independent confirmation that reported metrics, outcomes, baselines, methods, and claims are accurate enough for the intended use.

## Why It Matters

Blue Carbon verification is essential before ecological or social outcomes can be used for investor reporting, carbon revenue, KPI triggers, or coupon adjustments.
