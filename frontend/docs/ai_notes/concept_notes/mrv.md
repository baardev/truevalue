# MRV

## Definition

MRV means measurement, reporting, and verification. In Blue Carbon, it defines how carbon, ecosystem-service, livelihood, and other outcome claims are measured, reported, independently checked, and updated over time.

## Why It Matters

Without credible MRV, Blue Carbon claims cannot support outcome-linked KPIs, coupon triggers, carbon revenue, or investor-grade due diligence.
