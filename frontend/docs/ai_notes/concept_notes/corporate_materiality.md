# Corporate Materiality

## Definition

The boundary a corporation uses to decide which impacts, risks, or dependencies matter for reporting, governance, or investment decisions.

## Why It Matters

Materiality boundaries are useful for reporting, but they can remove system signals that matter before they become corporate risks.
