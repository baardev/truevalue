# Coupon Structure

## Definition

Coupon structure is the design of interest payments on a bond, including fixed coupons, step-up coupons, step-down coupons, blended or concessional coupons, guarantee-enhanced coupons, and outcome-linked coupon adjustments.

## Why It Matters

For Blue Carbon, coupon structure should not be finalized until KPI definitions, verification methods, market eligibility, risk sharing, and ecosystem-service evidence are credible.
