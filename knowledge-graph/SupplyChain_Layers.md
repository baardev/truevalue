# Supply Chain Layers

**Type:** rule (non-negotiable)

## The Three Layers

### Layer 1: Supply Chain (active)
Physical flow, custody, constraints. Phases 0-7.
If gold cannot be weighed, moved, or stored in a step, it does not belong here.

### Layer 2: Value Chain (deferred)
Profit, pricing, margins.
Introduced only after the physical chain is fully mapped.

### Layer 3: Financial Abstraction (deferred)
Paper claims, leverage, rehypothecation, derivatives.
Out of scope until supply chain and value chain are complete.

## Non-Negotiable Rule

Never mix layers prematurely. No pricing or value discussion during supply chain modeling. No economic inference without completed physical mapping.
